How to Export Inkscape Pages as Separate Files

Inkscape allows you to create and manage multi-page documents and export each page as an independent file in bulk. This guide provides a straightforward walkthrough on using the native Batch Export feature in Inkscape (version 1.2 and newer) to simultaneously save all document pages into individual formats such as PNG, SVG, PDF, or JPEG.

Step 1: Open the Export Dialog

  1. Open your multi-page document in Inkscape.
  2. Open the Export panel by navigating to File > Export... in the top menu bar, or press Ctrl + Shift + E (Windows/Linux) or Cmd + Shift + E (macOS).

Step 2: Switch to the Batch Export Tab

  1. In the Export panel on the right side of the screen, locate the tabs at the top.
  2. Click on the Batch export tab (adjacent to the "Single export" tab).

Step 3: Select the Pages Option

  1. Under the Batch export tab, you will see selection filters such as Layers, Pages, and Selection.
  2. Click on the Pages tab. A list showing all the pages in your document will appear, each with a checkbox next to it.
  3. Ensure the checkboxes for all the pages you wish to export are marked.

Step 4: Choose File Format and Settings

  1. At the bottom of the Batch export panel, select your desired export format from the dropdown menu (e.g., Portable Document Format (*.pdf), PNG (*.png), or Inkscape SVG (*.svg)).
  2. Adjust the resolution (DPI) or specific quality settings if prompted by the chosen format.

Step 5: Set Destination and Export

  1. Click the Directory icon (folder icon) next to the export path field to select the destination folder on your computer where the files will be saved.
  2. In the Suffix or file name template field, define how you want your files named (by default, Inkscape appends the page number or page label to the document name).
  3. Click the Export button at the bottom right.

Inkscape will process each page sequentially and save them as individual files in your chosen directory.

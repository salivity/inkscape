How to Preview Embroidery Stitch Simulations in Inkscape

Previewing embroidery stitch simulations in Inkscape allows you to inspect stitch patterns, check the sequencing of thread colors, and catch errors before transferring a design to an embroidery machine. This is accomplished using Ink/Stitch, the free, open-source embroidery extension for Inkscape. By rendering realistic needle paths and thread textures directly on screen, the simulator ensures your vector artwork translates accurately into machine-readable embroidery stitches.

Prerequisites

To simulate stitches, you must have the Ink/Stitch extension installed in Inkscape. If you have not installed it yet, download it from the official Ink/Stitch website and unpack it into your Inkscape user extensions folder, then restart the application.

Steps to Run the Stitch Simulator

  1. Open Your Design: Open your vector file in Inkscape and ensure your objects have been converted into embroidery elements (strokes for running stitches, fills for tatami or satin columns).
  2. Select the Artwork: Use the Select Tool (S or F1) to highlight the elements you want to preview. If you want to simulate the entire canvas, press Ctrl + A (or Cmd + A on macOS) to select all objects.
  3. Launch the Simulator: In the top menu bar, navigate to: Extensions > Ink/Stitch > Visualize and Export > Simulator/Realistic Preview
  4. Wait for Generation: A pop-up dialog will appear while Ink/Stitch calculates the stitch data. For complex or dense designs, this process may take several seconds.

Navigating the Simulation Window

Once the simulation window opens, you have access to several controls to examine the embroidery process:

  • Playback Controls: Use the Play/Pause button at the bottom of the window to watch the needle move across the fabric in real time.
  • Speed Slider: Adjust the playback speed to quickly scrub through large fill areas or slow down dense details.
  • Timeline Scrubbing: Click and drag anywhere along the bottom timeline to jump to a specific point in the stitch sequence.
  • Realistic View Toggle: Toggle the realistic rendering mode on or off to switch between a 3D textured thread view and a simplified wireframe line view.
  • Layer/Color Stepping: Use the forward and backward step buttons to skip directly between color changes, jump stitches, or trimmed sections.

Troubleshooting Simulation Errors

If the simulator fails to launch or displays an error message, an object in your design may violate embroidery formatting rules. To resolve this:

  1. Select your artwork.
  2. Go to Extensions > Ink/Stitch > Troubleshoot > Troubleshoot Objects.
  3. Check the canvas for warning markers highlighting intersecting paths, unsupported shapes, or unclosed nodes.
  4. Correct the identified vector paths and re-launch the simulator.

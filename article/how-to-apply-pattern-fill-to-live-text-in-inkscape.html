How to Apply Pattern Fill to Live Text in Inkscape

Applying a pattern fill directly to live text in Inkscape allows you to create intricate graphic typography without sacrificing editability. Instead of converting text to static vectors, you can use the Fill and Stroke dialog to assign stock or custom vector patterns straight to the text object, keeping the font, spelling, and kerning completely adjustable at any stage of your design workflow.

Step 1: Create and Select Your Live Text

  1. Select the Text Tool (T) from the left toolbar.
  2. Click on the canvas and type your text.
  3. Choose your desired font, size, and weight using the text settings in the top toolbar.
  4. Switch to the Select Tool (S) and click on your text to ensure it is selected.

Step 2: Open the Fill and Stroke Dialog

  1. Open the Fill and Stroke panel by pressing Ctrl + Shift + F (Windows/Linux) or Cmd + Shift + F (macOS), or by navigating to Object > Fill and Stroke in the top menu.
  2. In the panel that appears on the right, click on the Fill tab.

Step 3: Apply the Pattern Fill

  1. In the Fill tab, locate the row of fill type icons just below the tab name.
  2. Click the Pattern icon (represented by a small checkerboard pattern).
  3. Inkscape will automatically apply a default pattern to your text.
  4. Use the drop-down menu beneath the pattern button to browse and select from built-in patterns, such as stripes, checkerboards, polka dots, or camouflage.

Step 4: Adjust the Pattern Size, Angle, and Position

You can manipulate the pattern's alignment directly on the canvas while keeping the text editable:

  1. With the text selected, switch to the Node Tool (N).
  2. Look near the top-left corner of the canvas or the bounding box of your text to find the three pattern control handles:
    • The 'X' handle: Click and drag this handle to move the pattern's origin (repositioning).
    • The circular handle: Click and drag to rotate the pattern inside the text.
    • The square handle: Click and drag to scale the pattern larger or smaller. Holding Ctrl while dragging preserves the aspect ratio.

Optional: Use a Custom Pattern

If you want to use your own vector artwork as the pattern fill:

  1. Draw or import the vector graphic you want to turn into a pattern.
  2. Select the graphic and go to Object > Pattern > Objects to Pattern (or press Alt + I).
  3. Select your live text object.
  4. In the Fill and Stroke panel under the Fill tab, click the Pattern icon.
  5. Open the pattern drop-down menu and select your newly created pattern, which will appear at the top of the list.

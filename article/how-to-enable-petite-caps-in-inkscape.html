How to Enable Petite Caps in Inkscape

Inkscape provides robust support for advanced OpenType typography, allowing designers to access specialized glyph variants such as Petite Caps. Unlike standard Small Caps, which are typically slightly larger than the font's x-height, Petite Caps are specifically scaled to match the exact lowercase x-height for seamless typographic integration. This guide explains how to locate and activate the Petite Caps OpenType feature (pcap) using Inkscape’s native text formatting tools.

Prerequisites

To use Petite Caps, you must use a font that natively includes the OpenType Petite Capitals feature (such as Alegreya, Mrs Eaves, or EB Garamond). If a font does not contain these specific glyph variants, Inkscape cannot synthesize them automatically.

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Select Your Text:
    Use the Text tool (T) or the Select tool (S) to select the text object or highlight the specific portion of text you wish to format.

  2. Open the Text and Font Dialog:
    Go to the top menu and select Text > Text and Font..., or press the keyboard shortcut Shift + Ctrl + T (or Shift + Cmd + T on macOS).

  3. Navigate to the Features Tab:
    In the "Text and Font" panel that appears on the right side of the workspace, switch from the Font tab to the Features tab. This tab reveals all OpenType layout features supported by your selected font.

  4. Locate the Capitals Settings:
    Scroll down through the feature categories until you find the Capitals or Case section. Look for the Petite Capitals option (internally designated as the OpenType tag pcap).

  5. Enable Petite Caps:
    Check the box or toggle the switch next to Petite Capitals. If you only want lowercase letters converted, ensure you select Petite Capitals from Lowercase, or choose standard Petite Capitals depending on the font's implementation.

  6. Apply Changes:
    Click the Apply button at the bottom of the "Text and Font" panel to render the OpenType Petite Caps on your canvas.

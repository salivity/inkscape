How to Configure Auto-Save in Inkscape

This guide explains how to enable and configure the auto-save interval settings in Inkscape. Setting up auto-save helps prevent work loss during unexpected crashes or power failures by automatically creating backup copies of your vector graphics at regular, user-defined time intervals.

Step 1: Open the Preferences Menu

Launch Inkscape and open the Preferences dialog using one of the following methods:

  • On Windows and Linux, go to the top menu and select Edit > Preferences, or press Ctrl + Shift + P.
  • On macOS, select Inkscape > Preferences, or press Cmd + Shift + P.

Step 2: Navigate to the Autosave Settings

In the left sidebar of the Preferences window, locate the Input/Output section. Click the arrow next to it to expand the list, then select Autosave.

Step 3: Configure the Auto-Save Options

In the Autosave panel on the right, customize the following settings:

  1. Enable Autosave: Check the box next to Enable autosave (# of backups) to turn the feature on.
  2. Interval (in minutes): Enter the frequency at which Inkscape should save your project. For active projects, an interval between 5 and 10 minutes is recommended.
  3. Maximum number of autosaves: Set the maximum number of backup files Inkscape should keep. Once this limit is reached, Inkscape overwrites the oldest file.
  4. Autosave directory: By default, Inkscape saves files to your system's temporary directory. You can specify a custom folder path by clicking the browse icon to ensure your backups are stored in an easily accessible location.

Step 4: Save and Close

Inkscape applies preference changes automatically. Close the Preferences window to return to your canvas. Your work will now be automatically backed up according to the interval you defined.

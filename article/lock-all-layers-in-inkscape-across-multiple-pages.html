Lock All Layers in Inkscape Across Multiple Pages

Locking all layers across multiple pages in Inkscape is a straightforward process once you understand how Inkscape structures documents. Because layers in Inkscape are global and span across every page rather than being tied to an individual page, locking a layer automatically locks the content on that layer across your entire document. This guide explains how to select and lock every layer simultaneously using the Layers and Objects panel.

Understanding Layers Across Pages

In Inkscape (version 1.2 and newer), multi-page support shares the document's layer hierarchy. There are no page-specific layers; instead, objects on any given page simply reside on whichever layer they were assigned. Consequently, locking all document layers immediately protects your entire multi-page project from unintended edits.

Step-by-Step: Lock All Layers Simultaneously

  1. Open the Layers and Objects Panel Navigate to the top menu and select Layer > Layers and Objects..., or press the keyboard shortcut Ctrl + Shift + L (or Cmd + Shift + L on macOS).

  2. Select All Layers

    • Click on the top layer in the list to highlight it.
    • Scroll to the bottom of the layer list, hold down the Shift key, and click the last layer.
    • This action selects every layer in the document simultaneously. You can also hold Ctrl (or Cmd) to toggle specific layers into the selection.

  3. Toggle the Lock Icon

    • With all layers highlighted, click the Padlock icon next to any of the selected layers.
    • Clicking the lock will close the padlock for all selected layers at once.

Once locked, all vector paths, text elements, and embedded graphics across every page in the document are fully protected against selection, transformation, and deletion.

Alternative Method: Lock All Objects Directly

If you prefer to lock elements directly on the canvas without selecting through the panel:

  1. Press Ctrl + Alt + A to select all objects across all unlocked layers and all pages.
  2. Right-click any selected object on the canvas.
  3. Select Lock Selected Objects from the context menu (or press Shift + L depending on your shortcut configuration).

To reverse the process and resume editing, simply reselect all layers in the Layers and Objects panel and click the padlock icon again to unlock them all simultaneously.

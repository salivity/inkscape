How to Curve Straight Lines in Inkscape by Dragging

Bending a straight line into a curve is one of Inkscape's most intuitive features, allowing you to manipulate paths directly on the canvas without immediately fiddling with bezier handles. This guide explains how to use the Node tool to directly grab and pull any straight path segment into a smooth, custom curve with a simple click-and-drag motion.

Step 1: Select the Node Tool

To interact with the path segments directly, you must be in the correct editing mode.

  • Click the Edit paths by nodes tool from the left toolbar, or press the shortcut key N.
  • Click on the path or line you wish to modify to make its outline and nodes visible.

Step 2: Hover Over the Segment

Move your cursor over the straight segment between two nodes.

  • Watch for the cursor icon to change: the pointer will display a small curved line beneath it, indicating that the segment is ready to be manipulated directly.

Step 3: Click and Drag to Bend

  • Left-click and hold directly on the line segment.
  • Drag your mouse in the direction you want the curve to bow.
  • Release the mouse button once the curve reaches your desired shape.

What Happens Automatically

When you drag a straight segment, Inkscape automatically converts the adjacent corner nodes into smooth or cusp nodes with active bezier handles. If you need to refine the curve further, you can now click on the adjacent nodes and adjust the newly created handle levers for precision control.

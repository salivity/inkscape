How to Change Nudge Distance in Inkscape

Adjusting the arrow key nudge distance in Inkscape allows you to control exactly how far selected objects move with each keystroke. By default, Inkscape moves objects by a preset measurement, but you can easily customize this distance—and the unit of measurement—to match the precision needs of your design project. This guide outlines the exact steps to locate and modify this setting in Inkscape's preferences.

Steps to Adjust the Keyboard Nudge Distance

  1. Open the Preferences Menu

    • On Windows and Linux: Go to Edit > Preferences in the top menu bar, or press Ctrl + Shift + P.
    • On macOS: Go to Inkscape > Preferences, or press Cmd + Shift + P.

  2. Navigate to the Steps Settings

    • In the left-hand navigation pane of the Preferences window, click to expand the Behavior section.
    • Click on Steps.

  3. Modify the Nudge Distance

    • Locate the field labeled Arrow keys move by.
    • Enter your preferred numeric value.
    • Use the drop-down menu next to the field to select your desired unit of measurement (such as pixels px, millimeters mm, inches in, or points pt).

  4. Apply Changes

    • Inkscape saves preferences automatically. Close the Preferences window, and your new nudge distance will immediately apply to selected objects moved with the arrow keys.

Keyboard Modifiers for Nudging

Inkscape also supports built-in modifier keys to temporarily alter your nudge distance while working on the canvas:

  • Arrow Key: Moves the selection by the base distance defined in your preferences.
  • Shift + Arrow Key: Multiplies the base movement by 10 for faster adjustments.
  • Alt + Arrow Key: Moves the selection by exactly 1 screen pixel, regardless of your document zoom level.
  • Shift + Alt + Arrow Key: Moves the selection by 10 screen pixels.

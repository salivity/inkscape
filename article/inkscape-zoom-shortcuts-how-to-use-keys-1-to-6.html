Inkscape Zoom Shortcuts: How to Use Keys 1 to 6

Navigating the canvas efficiently is essential for a smooth vector design workflow in Inkscape. Rather than relying on the mouse wheel or the dedicated Zoom tool, you can instantly control your canvas magnification using the standard number keys 1 through 6. This guide provides a breakdown of each numerical shortcut, explaining exactly how they change your view so you can navigate complex projects with speed and precision.

Key 1: 1:1 Zoom (100% Scale)

Pressing 1 resets your canvas zoom to an exact 1:1 ratio (100%). This displays your artwork at its actual physical output size relative to your screen's resolution. It is best used for checking real-world proportions, fine-tuning typography, or verifying line weights.

Key 2: 1:2 Zoom (50% Scale)

Pressing 2 reduces the magnification to 1:2 (50%). This is a quick way to zoom out and get a broader perspective of your canvas without losing sight of the layout's structural details.

Key 3: Zoom to Selection

Pressing 3 centers and scales the viewport to fit the currently selected object or group of objects. If nothing is selected, this shortcut does nothing. It is particularly useful for quickly focusing on a specific path, node cluster, or design element you need to edit in detail.

Key 4: Zoom to Drawing

Pressing 4 adjusts the zoom level and centers the view so that every visual element currently on the canvas is visible. This ignores the document boundaries and focuses entirely on the drawn objects, making it an ideal shortcut when you have misplaced elements floating outside the page area.

Key 5: Zoom to Page

Pressing 5 centers and fits the entire document page into the viewport. This allows you to evaluate your overall layout, margins, and page composition at a glance, regardless of how far in or out you were working previously.

Key 6: Zoom to Page Width

Pressing 6 expands the view so that the width of the document page matches the width of your Inkscape window. This maximizes canvas space horizontally while maintaining your document's alignment, making it useful for banners, landscape designs, and wide-format layouts.

Usage Tip

To ensure these shortcuts register correctly, make sure you are not actively in text editing mode (the Text tool), as pressing number keys while editing will type the numbers into your text frame instead of zooming the canvas. Simply press Esc to deselect the text tool, then use numbers 1 through 6 to navigate.

How to Filter Objects by Name in Inkscape

Managing complex vector illustrations with dozens or hundreds of elements can quickly become overwhelming. This guide explains how to quickly locate and isolate specific elements by filtering their names or IDs using the search bar within Inkscape's Layers and Objects panel, streamlining your design workflow and saving valuable editing time.

Step 1: Open the Layers and Objects Panel

To access the panel, use one of the following methods:

  • Go to the top menu and select Layer > Layers and Objects... (or Object > Layers and Objects... depending on your Inkscape version).
  • Press the keyboard shortcut Ctrl + Shift + L.

The panel will open on the right side of your workspace, displaying the hierarchical tree of all layers, groups, and individual paths in the current document.

Step 2: Locate the Search Bar

At the top of the Layers and Objects panel, you will see a search input field marked with a magnifying glass icon. If the search bar is collapsed or hidden, look for the search/filter toggle icon in the panel's header bar to expand it.

Step 3: Enter the Object Name

  1. Click directly inside the search bar.
  2. Type the name, label, or ID of the object you wish to find.
  3. Inkscape dynamically filters the tree view in real time as you type. Elements that match your query will remain visible, while non-matching items are temporarily hidden from the list.

Step 4: Work with Filtered Results

  • Selection: Click any item in the filtered list to select it on the canvas. Hold Shift or Ctrl to select multiple filtered objects simultaneously.
  • Expanding Groups: If a matching object is nested inside a closed group or layer, the parent container will automatically expand to reveal the match.
  • Renaming: To make future filtering easier, right-click an object in the panel and select Object Properties... to change its ID, or double-click its label directly in the list to rename it.

Step 5: Clear the Filter

Once you have finished editing the isolated objects, click the X icon inside the search bar or delete the typed text. The Layers and Objects panel will immediately restore the full hierarchy of your document.

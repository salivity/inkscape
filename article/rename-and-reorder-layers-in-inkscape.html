Rename and Reorder Layers in Inkscape

Managing layers in Inkscape is essential for keeping complex vector artwork organized and easy to edit. This guide explains how to access the Layers and Objects dialog, rename individual layers for better clarity, and reorder their stacking positions to control how elements overlap in your document.

Open the Layers and Objects Dialog

Before modifying layers, ensure the dialog is visible:

  1. Click Layer in the top menu bar.
  2. Select Layers and Objects... from the dropdown menu.
  3. Alternatively, use the keyboard shortcut Ctrl + Shift + L (Windows/Linux) or Cmd + Shift + L (macOS).

The dialog will open as a dockable panel on the right side of the workspace.

How to Rename Layers

Giving layers descriptive names helps distinguish between background elements, foreground art, and design assets.

  1. In the Layers and Objects panel, locate the layer you want to rename.
  2. Double-click directly on the layer's name text.
  3. Type your desired name in the editable text field.
  4. Press Enter on your keyboard to confirm the change.

Alternatively, you can right-click the layer name and select Rename Layer..., enter the new name in the dialog box that appears, and click Rename (or press Enter).

How to Reorder Layers

The order of layers in the dialog determines the stacking order of elements on your canvas, with the top item in the list appearing in the very front of the design.

Method 1: Drag and Drop

  1. Click and hold the layer you want to move within the panel.
  2. Drag the layer up or down to its new position. A horizontal line indicator will show where the layer will be placed.
  3. Release the mouse button to drop the layer into place.

Method 2: Menu and Keyboard Shortcuts

Select the layer you wish to move, then use one of the following methods to shift its position:

  • Raise Layer (Move Up One Level): Go to Layer > Raise Layer or press Page Up.
  • Lower Layer (Move Down One Level): Go to Layer > Lower Layer or press Page Down.
  • Layer to Top (Move to Front): Go to Layer > Layer to Top or press Home.
  • Layer to Bottom (Move to Back): Go to Layer > Layer to Bottom or press End.

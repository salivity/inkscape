Move Ruler Origin to Bottom-Left in Inkscape

Starting with version 1.0, Inkscape changed its default coordinate system to place the (0,0) origin at the top-left corner to align with modern SVG specifications. This guide explains how to revert this behavior and configure Inkscape’s rulers so that the origin point sits at the bottom-left corner with the Y-axis pointing upward.

How to Change the Ruler Origin

To switch the origin point from the top-left to the bottom-left corner across your workspace, follow these steps:

  1. Open the Preferences Menu:

    • On Windows/Linux: Go to Edit > Preferences (or press Ctrl + Shift + P).
    • On macOS: Go to Inkscape > Preferences (or press Cmd + ,).

  2. Navigate to Interface Settings:

    • In the left sidebar of the Preferences window, select Interface.

  3. Modify the Coordinate Origin:

    • Look for the option labeled "Origin at top left with y-axis pointing down" (or "Origin at upper left with y-axis pointing down" depending on your exact version).
    • Uncheck this box.

  4. Apply the Changes:

    • The rulers will update automatically. Close the Preferences window.

Verifying the Change

Once unchecked, the horizontal ruler remains along the top or moves according to your settings, but the 0 mark for the vertical axis will align with the bottom edge of your document page. Increasing Y-values will now move upward toward the top of the canvas, matching the Cartesian coordinate system used in pre-1.0 versions of Inkscape. This setting persists across sessions and applies to newly opened documents.

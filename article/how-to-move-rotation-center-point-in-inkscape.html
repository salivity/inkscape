How to Move Rotation Center Point in Inkscape

Moving an object's rotation center in Inkscape allows you to change its pivot axis from the default geometric center to any custom point on or off the canvas. This guide provides a straightforward, step-by-step walkthrough on how to reveal, reposition, snap, and reset the rotation anchor point to achieve precise rotational transformations for your designs.

Step 1: Select the Object to Enter Rotation Mode

  1. Choose the Select and Transform Objects tool from the left toolbar (shortcut: S or F1).
  2. Click on the object you want to modify. You will see rectangular scaling arrows around the bounding box.
  3. Click the object a second time. The scaling arrows will change into curved rotation arrows at the corners and skew arrows on the sides.

Step 2: Locate the Rotation Anchor

Once rotation mode is active, locate the small crosshair (+) icon. By default, this rotation center anchor is placed directly in the geometric center of the selected object or group.

Step 3: Move the Rotation Center

  • Free Movement: Click and drag the crosshair anchor point to any location inside the object, outside the object, or anywhere on the canvas.
  • Precise Snapping: Turn on the Snap controls (located on the top-right toolbar or press %) to snap the rotation center to specific node points, grid intersections, path edges, or bounding box corners.

Step 4: Rotate Around the New Anchor Point

Click and drag any of the curved corner arrows. The object will now rotate around the newly positioned crosshair anchor point instead of its original center.

How to Reset the Rotation Center

If you need to return the rotation anchor to its default position:

  • Hold Shift and click directly on the crosshair anchor point. It will immediately snap back to the exact geometric center of the object.

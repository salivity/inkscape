How to Toggle the Selector Tool with Spacebar in Inkscape

In Inkscape, switching back and forth between object creation and object selection is a core part of the design workflow. This guide explains how to use the Spacebar shortcut to instantly toggle between the Selector tool and your most recently used active tool, helping you manipulate objects on the canvas quickly without having to click through tool menus or memorize multiple shortcut keys.

How the Spacebar Toggle Works

By default, Inkscape allows you to alternate between your active creation tool and the Selector tool with a single keypress.

  1. Select an Active Tool: Choose any creation or editing tool from the toolbox (such as the Bezier Pen, Rectangle, Circle, or Node tool).
  2. Press the Spacebar: Inkscape will immediately switch your cursor to the Selector tool, enabling you to select, move, resize, or rotate objects on your canvas.
  3. Press the Spacebar Again: Inkscape instantly switches back to the tool you were previously using with all of its settings preserved.

Important Exceptions

The Spacebar toggle works in most scenarios, but there are a few specific conditions where its behavior changes:

  • Active Text Editing: If you are using the Text tool and the cursor is actively blinking inside a text frame, pressing the Spacebar will type a blank space rather than switching tools. Press Esc first to finish editing text, after which the Spacebar can be used to toggle tools.
  • Mid-Stroke or Mid-Drag Actions: If you press the Spacebar while actively holding down the mouse button and drawing (such as with the Pencil or Calligraphy tool), Inkscape uses the Spacebar to "stamp" a copy of the shape onto the canvas instead of switching tools. Ensure your mouse button is released before pressing the Spacebar.

Verifying the Shortcut Setting

If the Spacebar does not toggle the Selector tool as expected, check your keyboard preferences:

  1. Open Edit > Preferences (or press Ctrl + Shift + P).
  2. Navigate to Interface > Keyboard.
  3. Verify that your active shortcut configuration uses the default Inkscape mapping, where the Spacebar is mapped to the tool toggle function.

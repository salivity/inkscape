Rotate Nodes Around a Center Point in Inkscape

This guide explains how to rotate a specific subset of nodes around a custom pivot point using Inkscape's Node tool. By enabling transform handles directly within node editing mode, you can reveal the rotation center crosshair, move it to any arbitrary location on your canvas, and precisely rotate only the selected path segments without affecting the rest of the object.

1. Enable Node Transform Handles

  1. Select your path and switch to the Node Tool by pressing N or clicking its icon in the left toolbar.
  2. Look at the Tool Controls Bar running horizontally along the top of the workspace.
  3. Locate and click the Show transform handles for selected nodes toggle button (its icon looks like a small bounding box with directional arrows).

2. Select the Subset of Nodes

  • Click and drag a selection box around the nodes you want to rotate, or hold Shift and click individual nodes to add them to your selection.
  • Once selected, a rectangular bounding box with scaling handles will appear strictly around those chosen nodes.

3. Switch to Rotation Mode

  • Click any one of the currently selected nodes a second time.
  • The arrow handles at the corners of the bounding box will change from straight scaling arrows to curved rotation arrows.
  • A small crosshair (+) will appear in the center of the bounding box. This crosshair represents the current pivot point.

4. Move the Pivot Point to an Arbitrary Location

  • Click and drag the central crosshair to wherever you want the center of rotation to be.
  • You can place this pivot point anywhere: on another node, at an empty coordinate on the canvas, or snapped to an intersection or guide line (ensure snapping is enabled in the top-right corner if you require exact alignment).

5. Rotate the Selected Nodes

  • Click and drag any of the curved corner handles to rotate the selected nodes around your repositioned pivot point.
  • To constrain the rotation to fixed 15-degree increments, hold Ctrl while dragging the handle.
  • Release the mouse button once the nodes reach your desired angle.

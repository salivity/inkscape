Inspect Out-of-Gamut Colors in Inkscape CMYK

This article explains how to set up and inspect out-of-gamut color warnings in Inkscape using CMYK soft-proofing. By configuring your color management settings, selecting a target print profile, and toggling soft-proof display controls, you can identify and adjust colors that fall outside the printable CMYK color space before sending your artwork to print.

1. Configure Color Management Preferences

Before checking for out-of-gamut colors, Inkscape must be linked to your target CMYK profile:

  1. Go to Edit > Preferences (or press Ctrl + Shift + P).
  2. Select the Color management tab from the left sidebar.
  3. In the Proofing profile dropdown, choose the CMYK ICC profile required by your commercial printer (such as Coated FOGRA39 or U.S. Web Coated SWOP). If your desired profile is missing, install the .icc file on your operating system and restart Inkscape.
  4. Set the Gamut warning color to a distinct, high-contrast color (such as bright magenta, cyan, or mid-gray) that stands out against your artwork.

2. Enable Soft-Proofing and Gamut Warning

Once your profiles are assigned, turn on the soft-proofing engine and the gamut check:

  1. Navigate to View > Color Display Mode in the top menu.
  2. Select Soft Proofing to simulate how the CMYK profile renders the colors on your display.
  3. To reveal colors that cannot be accurately reproduced in CMYK, go to View > Color Display Mode and toggle Gamut Check (or select the gamut warning option depending on your Inkscape version).

In Inkscape 1.2 and newer, you can also toggle soft-proofing and gamut check quickly by clicking the Color Management icon located at the bottom-right corner of the canvas window.

3. Identify and Correct Out-of-Gamut Colors

With Gamut Check enabled, any artwork element containing colors outside the CMYK gamut will be masked by the gamut warning color configured in your preferences.

To correct an affected object:

  1. Select the object displaying the gamut warning mask.
  2. Open the Fill and Stroke panel (Ctrl + Shift + F).
  3. Switch the color selector to the CMYK or HSL tab.
  4. Lower the saturation or manually adjust the CMYK sliders until the warning color disappears, ensuring the selected shade is safely within the printable gamut.

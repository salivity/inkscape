How to Insert Glyphs and Unicode in Inkscape

This guide explains how to use the built-in Glyphs dialog in Inkscape to browse, select, and insert special Unicode characters, symbols, and alternate font glyphs directly into your vector artwork. By leveraging this tool, you can access hidden font features, mathematical symbols, accented letters, and icons without needing to copy and paste them from external character maps.

Step 1: Create an Active Text Object

Before inserting any glyph, you must have an active text cursor on your canvas:

  1. Select the Text tool from the left toolbar (or press T / F8).
  2. Click anywhere on the canvas to create a new text object, or click inside an existing text block where you want the character to appear.

Step 2: Open the Glyphs Dialog

  1. Navigate to the top menu bar.
  2. Click on Text and select Glyphs... (or Unicode Characters... in some versions).
  3. The Glyphs panel will open as a dockable dialog on the right side of the workspace.

Step 3: Select Font and Filter Characters

Because glyph availability depends on the specific font you use, configure the dialog settings:

  1. Font Family: Set this to match the font applied to your text on the canvas.
  2. Font Style: Choose the appropriate weight or style (e.g., Regular, Bold, Italic).
  3. Script / Range: Use the dropdown menu to filter by specific Unicode blocks, such as Arrows, Currency Symbols, Mathematical Operators, or specific language scripts.

Step 4: Insert the Glyph

  1. Browse the character grid to find the symbol you need. Hovering over a character displays its Unicode name and hex code.
  2. Double-click the desired glyph, or select it and click the Append button at the bottom of the dialog.
  3. The character will instantly appear at your text cursor's position on the canvas.

Invert Mouse Wheel Zoom in Inkscape

This guide explains how to reverse the scroll wheel direction when zooming into and out of the canvas in Inkscape. By default, scrolling forward zooms in and scrolling backward zooms out, but you can quickly switch this behavior in the application preferences to match your preferred workflow or habits from other design software.

Steps to Invert the Scroll Wheel Zoom Direction

  1. Open Inkscape Preferences:

    • On Windows or Linux, click Edit in the top menu and select Preferences, or press Ctrl + Shift + P.
    • On macOS, click Inkscape in the menu bar and select Preferences, or press Cmd + Shift + P.

  2. Navigate to Mouse Settings:

    • In the left-hand sidebar of the Preferences window, expand the Interface category.
    • Click on Mouse (or Canvas, depending on your specific Inkscape version).

  3. Invert the Zoom Direction:

    • Locate the Zooming section.
    • Check the box labeled Invert zoom direction (or Invert mouse wheel zoom).

  4. Apply and Test:

    • Close the Preferences window. The changes take effect immediately without requiring you to restart Inkscape.
    • Return to your canvas, hold Ctrl (if not set to zoom by default), and scroll the wheel to verify the direction matches your preference.

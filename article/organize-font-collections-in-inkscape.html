Organize Font Collections in Inkscape

Managing a massive font library can slow down your design workflow, but Inkscape simplifies this through custom Font Collections introduced in recent versions. This guide provides a straightforward walkthrough on accessing the Text and Font dialog, creating dedicated font categories, assigning your typefaces to them, and filtering your collection for faster typography selection.

1. Open the Text and Font Dialog

To begin, launch Inkscape and open the font management panel:

  • Go to the top menu and select Text > Text and Font...
  • Alternatively, use the keyboard shortcut Shift + Ctrl + T.

2. Access the Font Collections Manager

Inside the Text and Font dialog, ensure you are on the Font tab:

  • Look near the top of the font list or next to the search bar for the Collections icon (represented by an edit/list icon).
  • Click this icon to toggle the Font Collections management interface.

3. Create a New Collection

  • Click the + (Add) button located in the collections area.
  • Type a name for your new collection (such as "Handwritten," "Client Project," or "Display Fonts") and press Enter to save it.

4. Assign Fonts to Your Collection

Once your collection is created, you can assign fonts to it:

  • Scroll through your main font list to find the font family you want to categorize.
  • Click and drag the font into your designated collection name, or right-click the font family and choose the option to assign it to your specific collection.
  • A single font can be assigned to multiple collections without duplicating any system files.

5. Filter Fonts While Designing

To use your organized collections:

  • Click the collection dropdown menu located above the font family list.
  • Select your custom collection. Inkscape will filter the font list to display only the typefaces in that category.
  • Choose your desired font and click Apply to style the selected text on your canvas.

6. Edit or Delete Collections

  • To remove a font from a collection, open the collection view, right-click the font name, and select the option to remove it.
  • To rename or delete an entire collection, right-click the collection label in the manager. Deleting a collection will only remove the grouping inside Inkscape; it will not delete or uninstall the fonts from your operating system.

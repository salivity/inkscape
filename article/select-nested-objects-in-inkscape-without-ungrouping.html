Select Nested Objects in Inkscape Without Ungrouping

Selecting specific elements buried inside complex, nested groups in Inkscape does not require you to dismantle your group hierarchy. By utilizing canvas modifier key shortcuts or the Layers and Objects panel, you can independently target, select, and modify multiple nested objects across different groups simultaneously while keeping your project structure completely intact.

Method 1: Using Canvas Keyboard Shortcuts

The fastest way to select multiple nested objects directly on the canvas is by combining the Ctrl and Shift modifier keys with the Select Tool (S).

  1. Activate the Select Tool by pressing S or F1.
  2. Hold down Ctrl and Shift simultaneously.
  3. Click on the first nested object you want to select. The Ctrl key instructs Inkscape to bypass the parent groups and target the specific shape beneath the cursor, while the Shift key adds the item to your selection.
  4. Continue holding Ctrl + Shift and click on any additional nested objects, regardless of which group or sub-group they belong to.

Tip: If an object is obscured beneath other layers or shapes, hold Ctrl + Alt + Shift and click repeatedly to cycle through the depth of overlapping nested objects while maintaining a multi-selection.

Method 2: Using the Layers and Objects Panel

When working with dense vector artwork where selecting on canvas is difficult, the dedicated tree-view panel provides full precision.

  1. Open the panel by navigating to Object > Layers and Objects or pressing Ctrl + Shift + L.
  2. Locate the parent groups in the list and click the arrows to expand the nested hierarchy.
  3. Hold the Ctrl key on your keyboard.
  4. Click each nested object you wish to select within the panel list.

Both methods immediately highlight the chosen elements with standard bounding boxes on the canvas, allowing you to change fill colors, apply strokes, transform paths, or move objects together without ungrouping the original artwork.

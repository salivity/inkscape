How to Snap to the Center of a Polygon in Inkscape

Snapping a line directly to the geometric center or center of gravity of a polygon in Inkscape is a straightforward process achieved by configuring the snapping toolbar and utilizing Inkscape's object center detection. This guide explains how to enable the necessary snapping targets, locate the true centroid of both regular and irregular polygons, and anchor a line precisely to that central point.

Step 1: Enable the Snapping Controls

  1. Ensure global snapping is enabled by clicking the Magnet icon in the top-right corner of the window (or press %).
  2. Open the snapping options menu by clicking the arrow next to the magnet icon (in Inkscape 1.2 and later) or referring to the Snap Controls Bar.
  3. Switch snapping to Advanced Mode if prompted.
  4. Check the box to enable Snap to Object Centers (ensure "Snap an item's center" or "Snap to bounding box center" is toggled on).

Step 2: Identify the Polygon's Geometric Center

  • Regular Polygons (created with the Stars and Polygons tool): Inkscape automatically maintains the geometric center as the object's rotational center.
  • Irregular Polygons (arbitrary shapes or converted paths): Inkscape’s default center snap targets the center of the bounding box. If your irregular polygon requires the true mathematical center of gravity (centroid):
    1. Select the polygon.
    2. Go to Extensions > Visualize Path > Measure Path.
    3. Set the measurement type to Centroid.
    4. Click Apply to generate a precise marker point at the true center of mass.
    5. In the snapping menu, ensure Snap to Nodes, Paths, and Handles is active to snap to this newly created point.

Step 3: Draw and Snap the Line

  1. Select the Draw Freehand Lines tool (P) or the Draw Bézier Curves and Straight Lines tool (B).
  2. Move your cursor near the middle of the polygon.
  3. Wait for the snapping tooltip to appear reading "Object center", "Item center", or "Cusp node" (if using the centroid marker).
  4. Click once to anchor the start of the line to the center point.
  5. Extend the line to your desired endpoint and double-click or press Enter to finish drawing.

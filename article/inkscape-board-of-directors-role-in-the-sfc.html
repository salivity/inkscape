Inkscape Board of Directors Role in the SFC

This article examines the role and purpose of the Inkscape Board of Directors within the Software Freedom Conservancy (SFC). It details how this governing committee manages community operations, directs financial resources, and guides project development while leveraging the legal and administrative infrastructure provided by the SFC.

The Relationship Between Inkscape and the SFC

Inkscape operates as a member project of the Software Freedom Conservancy, a 501(c)(3) non-profit organization that provides fiscal sponsorship and legal backing to open-source software projects. In this structure, the SFC serves as the legal and corporate umbrella for Inkscape. However, the SFC does not dictate the technical direction or internal policies of the software. Instead, day-to-day leadership is delegated to Inkscape's internal governing body, formally functioning as the Project Leadership Committee (PLC), commonly referred to as the Inkscape Board of Directors.

Key Purposes of the Inkscape Board

The Inkscape Board exists to represent the community's interests, manage resources, and ensure the project remains sustainable and autonomous. Its core responsibilities within the SFC framework include:

  • Budgetary and Financial Direction: While the SFC holds donations, grant funds, and assets in a dedicated account for Inkscape, the Inkscape Board determines how those funds are spent. The Board approves budgets for developer summits, bounties, infrastructure, and contractor positions, formally directing the SFC to execute payments and contracts.
  • Contractor and Employment Oversight: The Board identifies staffing needs—such as hiring dedicated developers, project managers, or user interface designers—and drafts terms of work. The SFC then handles the formal employment or independent contractor agreements, shielding individual community members from legal liabilities.
  • Trademark and Asset Stewardship: Inkscape’s trademarks, domain names, and hardware assets are legally assigned to the SFC for protection. The Board advises the SFC on appropriate usage policies and permissions, ensuring the Inkscape brand is preserved according to open-source values.
  • Liaison with Legal and Administrative Services: The Board acts as the official communication channel between the Inkscape community and the SFC’s legal team. When copyright, licensing, or compliance issues arise, the Board coordinates with the SFC to resolve them without burdening the general contributor community.
  • Strategic and Community Governance: The Board oversees general community guidelines, disputes, and contributor policies. It sets procedural frameworks that allow developers, translators, and designers to collaborate effectively without corporate interference.

Balance of Authority

The division of responsibility creates a balance of power:

  • The Software Freedom Conservancy ensures compliance with charity laws, maintains corporate status, manages tax filings, and provides legal protection against third-party claims.
  • The Inkscape Board of Directors maintains complete autonomy over project roadmaps, community guidelines, technical standards, and internal resource allocation.

This division allows Inkscape developers to focus entirely on software development and community growth, while the Board of Directors and the SFC handle the operational and legal overhead necessary to sustain a modern open-source application.

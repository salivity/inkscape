Keep Canvas Centered When Toggling Panels in Inkscape

Toggling side panels in Inkscape often shifts your artwork off-center because docked dialogs resize the active canvas area. You can prevent this distraction and maintain a centered workspace by adjusting your dialog display preferences to prevent canvas resizing, setting dialogs to floating mode, or utilizing automated viewport hotkeys.

Change Dialog Behavior to Floating

The most permanent way to stop panels from displacing the canvas center is to decouple them from the docking system so they do not alter the main viewport dimensions when opened or closed:

  1. Open the Preferences menu by going to Edit > Preferences (or press Ctrl + Shift + P on Windows/Linux, Cmd + Shift + P on macOS).
  2. In the left sidebar, expand the Interface category and select Windows.
  3. Locate the Dialog behavior section.
  4. Change the setting from Dockable to Floating.

With this setting enabled, tool panels (such as Fill and Stroke or Align and Distribute) will hover over the canvas in independent windows rather than docking into the side margin. When you open, minimize, or close these panels, the underlying canvas remains stationary and centered.

Toggle Docked Panels with Clean Redraws

If you prefer to keep panels docked into the sidebars rather than floating, the canvas must naturally resize to accommodate the panel width. To minimize disruption while maintaining proper centering:

  • Use the F12 Shortcut: Press F12 to hide or show all docked dialogs simultaneously. Toggling all docks at once prevents incremental shifting while you work.
  • Lock the Center with Quick-Center Shortcuts: If your artwork moves off-center after toggling panels via F12, you can instantly restore center alignment using Inkscape's built-in view hotkeys:
    • Press 5 to center and fit the entire page within the newly resized viewport.
    • Press 4 to center the entire drawing.
    • Press 3 to center your currently selected object.

Adjust Canvas Behavior Preferences

To fine-tune how the canvas interacts with UI changes:

  1. Go to Edit > Preferences > Interface > Canvas.
  2. Review the interaction settings for canvas panning and zooming. Ensure that smooth scrolling and zoom options are tuned to your preference so that canvas readjustments after panel toggles do not abruptly jump.

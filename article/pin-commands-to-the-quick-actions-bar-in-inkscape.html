Pin Commands to the Quick Actions Bar in Inkscape

This guide explains how to customize and pin your most frequently used actions to Inkscape’s Commands bar. By tailoring your toolbar settings, you can keep essential vector editing tools accessible with a single click, reduce menu navigation, and optimize your overall design workflow.

Understanding the Commands Bar

In Inkscape, the primary quick-action area is the Commands Bar. This toolbar houses standard shortcuts such as duplicate, group, align, export, and document properties. Depending on your version and layout settings, this bar sits either at the top of the workspace or along the right side.

How to Customize and Pin Commands

Inkscape allows you to show, hide, and reorganize actions on your toolbars via the global preferences menu.

  1. Open the Preferences Dialog

    • Go to Edit > Preferences from the top menu, or press Ctrl + Shift + P (Windows/Linux) or Cmd + Shift + P (macOS).

  2. Navigate to Toolbars Settings

    • In the left sidebar of the Preferences window, expand the Interface category.
    • Click on Toolbars.

  3. Select Your Visible Actions

    • Locate the section labeled Commands Bar.
    • You will see a checklist of every command available for the bar, including grouping, alignment, path operations, import/export, and object ordering.
    • Check the boxes next to the actions you use frequently to pin them to the bar.
    • Uncheck any rarely used commands to eliminate clutter and save screen real estate.

  4. Adjust Icon Sizing (Optional)

    • In the same Toolbars settings panel, adjust the Toolbars icon size slider to make your pinned action icons larger or smaller to fit your monitor resolution.

  5. Apply and Restart

    • Close the Preferences window. Most changes take effect immediately, but restarting Inkscape ensures all toolbar layout adjustments cache correctly.

Accessing Unpinned Commands with the Command Palette

For actions you use often but prefer not to clutter your toolbar with, use the built-in Command Palette:

  1. Press ? (Shift + /) on your keyboard while on the canvas.
  2. Type the name of the operation (such as "Simplify", "Trace Bitmap", or "Union").
  3. Press Enter to run the action instantly.

How to Plot Vector Paths with AxiDraw in Inkscape

This guide outlines the process of sending vector line paths directly from Inkscape to an AxiDraw pen plotter using the official AxiDraw software extension. You will learn how to properly prepare your vector document, configure the hardware setup, adjust plotting parameters, and execute the physical plot directly to your machine.

1. Prepare Your Vector Artwork

The AxiDraw plots strokes along vector paths. Ensure your artwork consists purely of clean, strokable paths rather than raster images or unstroked fills.

  • Convert Objects to Paths: Select all elements in your document and go to Path > Object to Path. If your artwork contains text, this step converts the text into vector outlines.
  • Ungroup Elements: Select everything and press Ctrl+Shift+G (or Cmd+Shift+G on macOS) repeatedly until no groups remain.
  • Verify Strokes: The pen plotter follows the center line of a vector stroke. Remove all fills from your shapes and assign a visible stroke color to confirm the exact line paths the pen will trace.
  • Set Document Dimensions: Go to File > Document Properties and set your page dimensions to match your physical paper size and the physical working area of your specific AxiDraw model (e.g., A4, A3, or custom dimensions). Ensure the units match (inches or millimeters). Place the artwork within the page boundaries.

2. Connect and Position the AxiDraw

  1. Connect the AxiDraw to your computer using the USB cable.
  2. Connect the barrel plug of the external power supply to the AxiDraw, then plug it into an electrical outlet.
  3. Manually position the AxiDraw carriage at the "Home" position (top-left corner of the plotting area), with the carriage physically aligned against the home stops.
  4. Mount your drawing tool (pen or pencil) into the tool holder at the proper height while the carriage is in the "pen up" state.

3. Open the AxiDraw Control Extension

In Inkscape, navigate to the top menu and select Extensions > AxiDraw > AxiDraw Control.... This will open the primary interface dialog.

4. Configure Plotting Parameters

Before executing the plot, configure your pen movement and mechanical settings:

  • Setup Tab: Use the "Toggle pen up/down" utility to test your pen placement. Verify that "pen down" touches the paper with appropriate pressure and "pen up" lifts the pen sufficiently clear of the drawing surface.
  • Options Tab: Set the Pen height: up and Pen height: down percentages based on your test. Adjust Pen raising speed and Pen lowering speed to prevent ink splatters.
  • Timing Tab: Adjust the Writing/Drawing speed (movement speed while drawing) and Transit speed (movement speed while moving between paths). Slower speeds yield higher precision, especially with delicate pen tips.

5. Execute the Plot

  1. Return to the Plot tab inside the AxiDraw Control dialog.
  2. Confirm the Plot radio button is selected.
  3. Click the Apply button in the lower-right corner.

The AxiDraw software will calculate the path trajectories and immediately begin transmitting motion commands over the USB connection, plotting the vector lines directly onto your paper.

Underline and Strikethrough Text in Inkscape

This guide demonstrates how to apply underline and strikethrough styling to your text using the built-in Text tool controls in Inkscape. By using the dedicated controls bar at the top of the workspace, you can style complete text objects or highlight individual words quickly without opening extra formatting dialogs.

Step 1: Activate the Text Tool

Select the Text tool from the toolbox on the left side of the screen (or press T / F8 on your keyboard).

Step 2: Select the Text

  • To style an entire block of text, click once on the text object with the Text tool active.
  • To style a specific word or phrase, click and drag your cursor over the exact characters you want to modify to highlight them.

Step 3: Use the Tool Controls Bar

Look at the Tool Controls Bar positioned directly above the main canvas area. When the Text tool is active, this bar displays typography settings such as font family, font size, alignment, and style modifiers.

  • To add an Underline: Click the Underline button (an icon displaying a letter with a line beneath it, such as U).
  • To add a Strikethrough: Click the Strikethrough button (an icon displaying a letter with a horizontal line cutting through its center, such as S).

Toggling and Removing Styles

Both the underline and strikethrough controls function as toggles. To remove the styling, select the modified text again and click the active button on the Tool Controls Bar to turn it off. You can apply both effects simultaneously to the same selection if needed.

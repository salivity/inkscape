Paste Inkscape Vectors into LibreOffice Writer

Pasting vector artwork from Inkscape directly into LibreOffice Writer often results in a flattened, rasterized image if done incorrectly. By utilizing the Paste Special feature and converting the resulting graphic, you can preserve the vector properties of your Inkscape designs and treat them as native, scalable drawing shapes directly within your Writer document.

Step 1: Copy the Vector Objects in Inkscape

  1. Open your project in Inkscape.
  2. Use the Select Tool (S or F1) to highlight the specific paths or groups you want to transfer.
  3. Copy the selection to your clipboard by pressing Ctrl + C (or Cmd + C on macOS).

Step 2: Use Paste Special in LibreOffice Writer

Standard pasting (Ctrl + V) frequently defaults to a bitmap format like PNG. To maintain vector quality:

  1. Switch to your LibreOffice Writer document and place your cursor where you want the graphic.
  2. Open the Paste Special menu by pressing Ctrl + Shift + V (or go to Edit > Paste Special > Paste Special...).
  3. From the list of options, select Scalable Vector Graphics (SVG) and click OK.

Your artwork will now appear in LibreOffice Writer as a sharp, resolution-independent vector image.

Step 3: Convert the Graphic to Native Shapes

If you need to edit individual anchor points, fills, or strokes directly inside LibreOffice, you must convert the SVG object into native drawing shapes:

  1. Right-click the newly pasted SVG image in Writer.
  2. Navigate to Convert in the context menu.
  3. Select To Curve or To Polygon.

Once converted, right-click the object and choose Break (or Group > Ungroup). The vector design is now split into individual LibreOffice shape elements that can be recolored, resized, and modified without loss of quality.

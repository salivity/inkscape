How to Deselect a Single Object in Inkscape

When working with complex vector graphics in Inkscape, managing multiple selections efficiently is essential for a smooth workflow. This guide explains the exact keyboard shortcut used to deselect a single object from an existing multi-selection without clearing your entire selection, saving you from having to reselect your items from scratch.

The Deselect Shortcut

To deselect a single object from a multiple-selection group in Inkscape, hold down the Shift key and left-click directly on the object you want to remove.

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Activate the Select Tool: Ensure you are using the Select and Transform Objects tool by pressing S or F1, or by clicking the black arrow icon at the top of the left-hand toolbar.
  2. Identify the Target Object: Locate the specific element within your active selection that you want to exclude.
  3. Shift + Click to Deselect: Hold down the Shift key on your keyboard and click on the selected object.

The bounding box around that specific item will disappear, while all other selected objects remain actively selected.

How the Shift Key Works in Inkscape

In Inkscape, the Shift key functions as a selection toggle:

  • Clicking an unselected object while holding Shift adds it to your current selection.
  • Clicking an already-selected object while holding Shift subtracts it from your current selection.

Deselecting Objects Inside a Group

If the object you want to deselect is part of an SVG group rather than an ad-hoc multi-selection, you must hold Ctrl + Shift and left-click the object. This allows you to target and toggle individual items nested directly inside a group hierarchy without ungrouping them.

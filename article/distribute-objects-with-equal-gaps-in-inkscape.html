Distribute Objects with Equal Gaps in Inkscape

This guide explains how to evenly distribute objects in Inkscape based on the physical gaps between their bounding box edges rather than their center points. By utilizing the dedicated spacing options within the Align and Distribute panel, you can maintain consistent negative space between elements of varying dimensions across horizontal or vertical axes.

Step 1: Open the Align and Distribute Panel

Open the distribution controls by pressing Shift + Ctrl + A (or Cmd + Shift + A on macOS). You can also access this panel by navigating to the main menu and selecting Object > Align and Distribute.

Step 2: Select the Objects

Using the Selector Tool (shortcut: S), click and drag a selection box around the objects you want to rearrange, or hold Shift and click each object individually. You need to select at least three objects to distribute spacing between them. The outermost objects will remain in place to define the total boundary, while the inner objects will shift automatically.

Step 3: Apply Equal Gap Distribution

In the Align and Distribute panel, scroll down to the Distribute section. Look for the options that measure empty space rather than object anchors:

  • For Horizontal Gaps: Click the Make horizontal gaps between objects equal button. Inkscape will calculate the empty space between the right edge of one bounding box and the left edge of the next, adjusting all intermediate objects so every gap is identical.
  • For Vertical Gaps: Click the Make vertical gaps between objects equal button. Inkscape will balance the negative space between the bottom edge of each bounding box and the top edge of the one below it.

Step 4: Adjust Bounding Box Settings (Optional)

If your gaps look uneven, Inkscape may be calculating distances using paths rather than the visible strokes. You can change how bounding boxes are measured:

  1. Go to Edit > Preferences (Inkscape > Preferences on macOS).
  2. Select Tools from the left-hand menu.
  3. Under Bounding box to use, select Visual bounding box to ensure stroke widths and edge blurs are factored into the gap distance. Select Geometric bounding box if you only want the raw path coordinates to define the edges.

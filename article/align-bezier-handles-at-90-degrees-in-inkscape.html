Align Bezier Handles at 90 Degrees in Inkscape

This guide explains how to accurately align two adjacent Bezier handles to form a precise 90-degree right angle in Inkscape. By converting the target node to a corner node and utilizing Inkscape’s built-in angle constraints or guide snapping, you can achieve mathematical precision without relying on visual estimation.

Method 1: Using the Ctrl Key Angle Constraint

Inkscape includes a built-in angular constraint feature that locks handle movement to specific degree increments (15 degrees by default). Since 90 is a multiple of 15, this is the fastest way to create a right angle.

  1. Select the Node Tool: Press N or select the Edit paths by nodes tool from the left toolbar.
  2. Convert to a Corner Node: Click on the node shared by the two handles. On the top toolbar, click the Make selected nodes corner icon (or press Shift + C). This uncouples the handles so they can move independently.
  3. Adjust the First Handle: Click and drag the first Bezier handle while holding down Ctrl. Release the mouse when the handle is aligned to your desired baseline angle (such as 0°, 45°, or 90°).
  4. Adjust the Second Handle: Hold Ctrl and drag the adjacent Bezier handle. Rotate it until it snaps precisely six increments (90 degrees) away from the first handle.

Method 2: Snapping to Guides for Arbitrary Rotations

If your path is oriented at an irregular angle that does not align with 15-degree increments, use snapping guides.

  1. Enable Snapping: Ensure global snapping is active (toggle in the top-right corner).
  2. Enable Handle and Guide Snapping: Open the Snap Controls popover, ensure Snap to guides is enabled, and under the Advanced/Nodes section, ensure Snap to Bezier handles is turned on.
  3. Create a 90-Degree Guide Intersection: Drag two guides from the rulers onto your canvas. Double-click the second guide to manually set its angle to 90 degrees relative to the first guide, or place it perpendicular to the base angle.
  4. Snap the Node and Handles: Move your node to the intersection point of the guides. Hold Shift while dragging each handle along its respective guide axis until it snaps directly to the guide line.

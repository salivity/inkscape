How to Hide All Toolbars in Inkscape for a Clean Canvas

This guide explains how to quickly maximize your workspace and toggle user interface elements in Inkscape. While working on complex vector graphics, UI toolbars, panels, and menus can clutter the screen. Below, you will learn the primary keyboard shortcuts used to hide dialogs and switch to fullscreen view, how to hide toolbars manually, and how to assign a custom shortcut to achieve a completely clean canvas.

Key Shortcuts for Canvas Visibility

Inkscape does not feature a single, out-of-the-box shortcut that hides every single toolbar at once (like the Tab key in programs like Photoshop or GIMP). Instead, it splits canvas management across two primary keyboard shortcuts:

  • F12 (Toggle Dialog Visibility): Instantly hides or reveals all open dockable dialogs, panels, and sidebars on your screen. This immediately reclaims the horizontal space around your artwork.
  • F11 (Toggle Fullscreen Mode): Expands the Inkscape window to fill the entire monitor, eliminating the operating system title bar and taskbar for a distraction-free experience.

Using F11 combined with F12 provides the fastest default method to eliminate window distractions and clear away heavy panels.

Hiding Toolbars via the View Menu

If you want to remove the toolbars themselves (such as the Toolbox, Commands Bar, Tool Controls Bar, Snap Controls Bar, and Statusbar), you can toggle them individually:

  1. Click on View in the top menu bar.
  2. Hover over Show/Hide.
  3. Uncheck the toolbars you wish to conceal:
    • Menu Bar
    • Commands Bar
    • Snap Bar
    • Tool Controls Bar
    • Toolbox
    • Rulers
    • Scrollbars
    • Statusbar

Once hidden, your settings will persist across sessions until you re-enable them through the same menu.

Setting a Custom Shortcut for a Clean Canvas

To hide and show specific toolbars using a single key press, you can assign custom shortcuts in Inkscape:

  1. Go to Edit > Preferences (or press Ctrl + Shift + P).
  2. Select Interface > Keyboard from the sidebar.
  3. In the search box, type the name of the toolbar you want to toggle (e.g., "Toolbox" or "Tool Controls").
  4. Click on the command under the Shortcut column and press your preferred key combination.
  5. Close the preferences window to immediately use your new shortcut.

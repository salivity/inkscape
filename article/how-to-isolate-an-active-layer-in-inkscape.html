How to Isolate an Active Layer in Inkscape

Isolating an active layer in Inkscape allows you to work on specific artwork without accidentally selecting, moving, or modifying objects on other layers. While Inkscape does not feature a dedicated single-click "solo" button, you can achieve complete layer isolation by using the built-in locking and hiding functions within the Layers panel.

Step 1: Open the Layers and Objects Panel

To manage your layers, first open the Layers dialog:

  • Go to the top menu and select Layer > Layers and Objects...
  • Alternatively, press the keyboard shortcut Ctrl + Shift + L (or Cmd + Shift + L on macOS).

Step 2: Select Your Active Layer

In the Layers and Objects panel, locate the list of layers in your document. Click on the layer you want to isolate to make it your current active layer.

Step 3: Lock All Other Layers

To prevent objects on other layers from being selected while keeping them visible as a reference:

  1. Right-click on the name of your active layer in the panel.
  2. Select Lock Other Layers from the context menu.

Every layer except your selected active layer will display a closed padlock icon, making their elements unselectable on the canvas.

Step 4: Hide Other Layers (Optional)

If you prefer total visual isolation with zero distractions:

  1. Right-click on your active layer in the panel.
  2. Select Hide Other Layers from the context menu.

This toggles off visibility for all surrounding layers, leaving only the active layer visible on your workspace.

How to Restore All Layers

Once you have finished editing the isolated layer, right-click any layer in the Layers and Objects panel and select Unlock All Layers (or Unhide All Layers) to return your workspace to its normal state.

How to Show Inkscape Coordinates in Millimeters

This guide explains how to configure Inkscape's status bar to display real-time cursor coordinates in millimeters instead of the default pixels or inches. Inkscape ties the coordinate readouts in the bottom status bar directly to the document's active display units, meaning you can adjust this behavior instantly through either the Document Properties menu or by interacting with the canvas rulers.

Method 1: Using Document Properties

  1. Open your project in Inkscape.
  2. Open the Document Properties window by going to File > Document Properties, or by pressing Ctrl + Shift + D (Windows/Linux) or Cmd + Shift + D (macOS).
  3. In the dialog box that appears, ensure you are on the Page tab.
  4. Locate the Display units dropdown menu (located near the top of the settings, just below the general display options).
  5. Click the dropdown and select mm.
  6. Close the Document Properties panel.

Method 2: Using the Canvas Rulers

  1. Ensure the rulers are visible around your canvas. If they are hidden, press Ctrl + R (or Cmd + R) or go to View > Show/Hide > Rulers.
  2. Right-click directly on either the horizontal or vertical ruler at the edge of the workspace.
  3. Select mm from the contextual menu.

Verifying the Status Bar Output

Once the display units are set to millimeters, look at the bottom-right corner of the Inkscape interface. As you move your mouse across the canvas, the X and Y fields in the status bar will now calculate and display your cursor's exact position in millimeters.

How to Flip Canvas View Horizontally in Inkscape

Flipping the canvas horizontally is an essential technique for digital artists and designers to check proportions, balance, and symmetry without permanently modifying the actual artwork. Modern versions of Inkscape include a built-in canvas flipping feature that mirrors only your perspective, leaving the underlying vector paths intact. This guide explains how to flip the canvas view using the main menu, use the bottom status bar, set up a custom shortcut for a faster workflow, and quickly reset the view back to normal.

Flip the Canvas View via the Menu Bar

The most direct way to mirror your canvas view without affecting your objects is through the application menu:

  1. Open your project in Inkscape.
  2. Go to the top menu and click View.
  3. Hover over Canvas Orientation.
  4. Click Flip Horizontal.

Your entire workspace viewport will immediately mirror horizontally, allowing you to spot anatomical or symmetrical inconsistencies.

Flip the Canvas Using the Status Bar

You can also control the canvas orientation directly from the interface controls:

  1. Look at the bottom-right corner of the Inkscape window on the Status Bar.
  2. Locate the rotation and canvas management controls next to the zoom percentage indicator.
  3. Click the canvas orientation dropdown or use the dedicated flip toggle button to instantly mirror your display.

Set a Custom Keyboard Shortcut for Quick Symmetry Checks

Checking symmetry frequently during a drawing session is much faster with a dedicated hotkey:

  1. Go to Edit > Preferences (or press Ctrl + Shift + P).
  2. In the left panel, select Interface and then click Keyboard Shortcuts.
  3. In the search field at the top, type Flip Horizontal.
  4. Locate the entry under the View or Canvas category (make sure to choose the canvas view flip, not the object flip command).
  5. Click in the shortcut column for that action and press your desired key combination (for example, Alt + F or Shift + H).
  6. Close the Preferences window to start using your shortcut instantly while drawing.

How to Reset the Canvas View to Normal

Once you have verified your drawing's symmetry, you can easily return to the standard view:

  • Via the Menu: Go to View > Canvas Orientation > Reset Flip or select Default Orientation.
  • Via Keyboard: Press Ctrl + 1 (or 1 depending on your version) to reset zoom and orientation to standard parameters.
  • Via the Status Bar: Click the reset orientation icon next to the canvas rotation angle field in the bottom-right corner.

Customize Snap Bar Position in Inkscape

This guide explains how to reposition the snapping controls bar along the edges of your canvas in Inkscape. Whether you are using modern releases featuring the compact snapping popover or prefer a persistent toolbar pinned to the top, bottom, or sides of the workspace, the following steps detail how to switch layout modes and adjust interface preferences to place the snap bar exactly where you need it.

Enable the Permanent Snap Toolbar (Inkscape 1.2 and Newer)

Recent versions of Inkscape consolidate snapping tools into a single popover button located in the top-right corner of the window by default. To turn this popover into an edge-docked toolbar:

  1. Click the Snap Controls icon (the magnet icon) in the upper-right corner to open the snap menu.
  2. Click the small arrow or open the advanced options menu within the popover.
  3. Select Permanent Snap Bar (or check the box for persistent display). This detaches the options from the dropdown and places them as a dedicated bar along the canvas edge.

Move the Snap Bar Using View Layouts

Inkscape provides preset interface layouts that automatically reposition primary toolbars, including the snap controls, to different edges of the screen.

  1. Click View in the top menu bar.
  2. Navigate down to the layout presets section:
    • Default: Places primary controls and snap options along the top and right edges.
    • Custom: Optimizes toolbar placement for custom workspace scaling, usually positioning controls across the top.
    • Wide: Repositions toolbars along the right and left vertical borders to maximize vertical canvas space on widescreen monitors.
  3. Select the layout that corresponds to your preferred edge orientation.

Adjust Snap Bar Positioning in Preferences

For precise control over which toolbars appear and how interface elements are ordered along the borders:

  1. Open Edit > Preferences (or press Ctrl + Shift + P on Windows/Linux, Cmd + Shift + P on macOS).
  2. Expand the Interface category in the left sidebar.
  3. Click on Toolbars.
  4. Locate the Snap toolbar entry. Here, you can adjust its visibility and sizing options.
  5. In the same menu under Interface, select Windows to configure whether toolbars can be freely moved or locked to the main application window edges.

Repositioning the Toolbar Handle

If your current layout configuration allows toolbar docking:

  1. Locate the thin grip handle (a series of dots or lines) at the far end of the snap toolbar.
  2. Click and hold the handle with your mouse cursor.
  3. Drag the toolbar toward the top, bottom, left, or right border of the canvas until a colored highlight indicates a valid docking location.
  4. Release the mouse button to lock the snap bar into its new position.

How to Filter Glyphs by Unicode Block in Inkscape

This guide explains how to locate and insert special characters using the Glyphs dialog in Inkscape by filtering them according to specific Unicode blocks. You will learn how to access the Glyphs panel, select the appropriate character range—such as Currency Symbols, Arrows, or Mathematical Operators—and insert the desired symbols directly into your vector artwork.

Step 1: Open the Glyphs Dialog

  1. Launch Inkscape and open your document.
  2. Select the Text Tool (T or F8) from the left toolbar.
  3. Click anywhere on the canvas to create an active text cursor, or select an existing text object where you want the character to appear.
  4. Go to the top menu bar, click on Text, and select Glyphs (or Text > Glyphs...). This opens the Glyphs dock on the right side of the workspace.

Step 2: Choose Your Font

Unicode support varies depending on the typeface you are using. To ensure your desired symbols are available:

  1. In the Glyphs panel, set the Family dropdown to your target font (e.g., Arial, Noto Sans, or a specialized symbol font).
  2. Set the Face dropdown to the desired style (e.g., Regular, Bold).

Step 3: Filter by Unicode Block (Range)

By default, the panel may display all characters or a common set. To narrow down the list:

  1. Locate the Range dropdown menu inside the Glyphs panel.
  2. Click the dropdown to view the list of standardized Unicode blocks.
  3. Scroll through the list and select the block you need. Common blocks include:
    • Basic Latin or Latin-1 Supplement (accented characters, common symbols)
    • Currency Symbols (Euro, Yen, Pound, etc.)
    • Arrows (directional vectors)
    • Mathematical Operators (infinity, square roots, plus-minus)
    • Geometric Shapes (squares, circles, triangles)
    • Box Drawing or Dingbats
  4. Once selected, the grid beneath the menu will refresh to show only the characters belonging to that specific Unicode block.

Step 4: Insert the Character

  1. Browse the filtered grid to find the symbol you want to use.
  2. Double-click the glyph, or select it and click the Append button at the bottom of the panel.
  3. The selected character will immediately be inserted at your active text cursor's position on the canvas.

Show Real-Time Cursor Coordinates in Inkscape

This guide explains how to monitor cursor positions and display live coordinate information while working in Inkscape. While Inkscape primarily streams real-time X and Y pointer coordinates through the application's bottom Status Bar, users needing direct on-canvas visual readouts can utilize specific built-in tools—such as the Measure Tool, live snapping tooltips, and geometric node displays—to view positional data directly over their artwork.

Viewing Real-Time Coordinates in the Status Bar

By default, Inkscape constantly updates your cursor's exact coordinates in the workspace interface:

  1. Look at the Status Bar located at the very bottom of the Inkscape window.
  2. Locate the X: and Y: coordinate fields (typically situated toward the bottom right or center, next to the zoom and rotation controls).
  3. Move your mouse across the canvas; the X and Y indicators update instantaneously according to your cursor's position relative to the document origin.
  4. To change the display measurement units (e.g., millimeters, pixels, or inches), press Ctrl + Shift + D to open Document Properties and change the Display units setting.

Displaying On-Canvas Overlays with the Measure Tool

If you require coordinates and dimensional data directly on the canvas surface rather than at the bottom of the screen, the Measure Tool acts as a real-time overlay:

  1. Press M or select the Measure Tool (ruler icon) from the left toolbox.
  2. Click and hold anywhere on your canvas, then drag the cursor.
  3. Inkscape will project a real-time visual overlay following the cursor, displaying:
    • Current distance (length).
    • Angle relative to the horizontal axis.
    • Coordinate offsets (\(\Delta X\) and \(\Delta Y\)).
  4. To measure relative to existing objects without creating permanent paths, hover over shapes with the Measure Tool enabled to view automatic surface-to-point overlays.

Enabling On-Canvas Snapping Tooltips

Inkscape can display temporary on-canvas tooltips directly at the cursor when it aligns with specific coordinates or geometries:

  1. Ensure the Snapping control bar is enabled (toggle the magnet icon in the top-right corner or press %).
  2. Expand the snapping popover menu and enable Snap to Grid, Snap to Guides, or Snap to Paths.
  3. Move your cursor near canvas elements or grid lines. Inkscape will generate an on-canvas pop-up tag directly at the cursor tip indicating the exact structural coordinate being targeted (e.g., "cusp node", "intersection", or "grid line").

Checking Coordinates with the Node Tool

For precise placement and coordinate inspection of vector paths:

  1. Press N to switch to the Node Tool.
  2. Select any vector path to reveal its nodes.
  3. Click on a specific node. The Tool Controls Bar at the top of the screen will immediately display the exact absolute X and Y coordinates for that specific point.
  4. Dragging a node displays a real-time movement delta overlay indicating how far the point has moved from its original coordinates.

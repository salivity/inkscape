How to Make a Circular Cusp in Inkscape

This guide explains how to convert a straight line segment between two nodes into a circular cusp using Inkscape. By utilizing the Node Tool, adjusting segment curvature, and manipulating node handles into corner (cusp) states, you can quickly reshape flat vector paths into precise, sharp-pointed circular curves.

Step 1: Select the Node Tool

  1. Open your document in Inkscape and select the path containing the straight line segment.
  2. Switch to the Node Tool by pressing N on your keyboard or selecting the Node Tool icon from the left-hand toolbar.

Step 2: Convert the Segment to a Curve

  1. Hover your cursor directly over the straight line segment between the two target nodes.
  2. Click and drag the segment outward. Inkscape automatically converts the straight line into a Bezier curve as you drag.
  3. Alternatively, click the segment to select the two bounding nodes, then click the Make selected segments curves icon in the top Tool Controls Bar.

Step 3: Set the Nodes to Cusp (Corner) Type

A cusp node allows control handles to move independently, creating a sharp corner rather than a smooth transition.

  1. Click and drag a selection box around the nodes bounding the segment, or hold Shift and click each node individually.
  2. In the top Tool Controls Bar, click the Make selected nodes corner button (icon showing a sharp corner angle), or press Shift + C.

Step 4: Shape the Circular Arc

  1. Click the node you wish to refine to reveal its Bezier handle.
  2. Drag the handle outward to shape the curve.
  3. To achieve a true circular arc, adjust the handle so it extends perpendicular to the radius of your intended circle. The ideal handle length for approximating a 90-degree circular arc is approximately 55.2% of the circle's radius.
  4. If you want a pointed cusp along the segment itself, double-click the curved line segment to insert a new node at the apex, set that node to Corner (Shift + C), and bend both adjacent curve handles symmetrically to form a sharp, circular cusp point.

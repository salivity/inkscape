How to Apply a Dashed Stroke in Inkscape

Applying a dashed stroke style to a path in Inkscape is a straightforward process managed through the Fill and Stroke panel. By accessing the stroke style settings, you can convert any solid vector line into a customized dashed or dotted pattern, adjust the spacing, and change the dash offset to suit your design requirements.

Step 1: Select Your Path

Using the Select and Transform objects tool (shortcut: S) or the Edit paths by nodes tool (shortcut: N), click on the path or object to which you want to apply the dashed stroke.

Step 2: Open the Fill and Stroke Dialog

If the panel is not already visible on the right side of your workspace, open it using one of the following methods:

  • Press Shift + Ctrl + F (Windows/Linux) or Shift + Cmd + F (macOS).
  • Navigate to the top menu and select Object > Fill and Stroke...

Step 3: Ensure Stroke Paint is Enabled

  1. In the Fill and Stroke panel, click on the Stroke paint tab.
  2. Ensure the stroke is set to a visible color by selecting the Flat color icon (the solid square next to the "X").

Step 4: Choose a Dashed Style

  1. Switch to the Stroke style tab inside the Fill and Stroke panel.
  2. Locate the Dashes dropdown menu.
  3. Click the dropdown to open the list of preset dash and dot patterns.
  4. Select your preferred dashed pattern from the list. The changes will immediately appear on your selected path.

Step 5: Customize Dash Properties (Optional)

To refine the appearance of your dashed line:

  • Width: Adjust the thickness of the line using the Width numerical field. Changing this will proportionally scale the size of the dashes and the gaps between them.
  • Dash Offset: Use the offset box next to the dash selector to shift the starting position of the dash pattern along the path.
  • Cap Style: Choose between Butt cap, Round cap, or Square cap to change the shape of the ends of each individual dash.
  • Join Style: Select how the corners appear if your path has sharp angles (Miter, Round, or Bevel).

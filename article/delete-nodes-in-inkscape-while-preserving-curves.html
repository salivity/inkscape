Delete Nodes in Inkscape While Preserving Curves

When editing vector paths, removing redundant points without ruining the shape of your artwork is essential for clean design work. Inkscape includes a built-in curve-preservation feature that automatically recalculates adjacent control handles when a node is deleted, keeping the path geometry intact. This guide explains the exact shortcut to perform this action and how to ensure your Bezier handles adjust correctly.

The Curve-Preserving Deletion Method

To delete a node while forcing Inkscape to recalculate adjacent handles to maintain the curve, use standard deletion:

  1. Switch to the Node Tool by pressing N or selecting the Edit paths by nodes icon from the left-hand toolbar.
  2. Click on the path to reveal its nodes.
  3. Click the specific node you wish to remove to select it (it will change color to indicate selection).
  4. Press Delete or Backspace on your keyboard.

Inkscape's default behavior for Delete and Backspace is to remove the chosen node and immediately adjust the Bezier handles of the preceding and succeeding nodes to match the original curvature as closely as mathematically possible.

Contrast: Deleting Without Preserving Curves

If you want to remove a node without recalculating the handles—creating a straight segment or leaving adjacent handles untouched—hold Ctrl while deleting:

  • Press Ctrl + Delete (or Ctrl + Backspace).

Understanding this contrast confirms whether your software is behaving normally. If pressing Delete flattens your curve, your keyboard preferences may be inverted, and pressing Ctrl + Delete will trigger the curve-preserving calculation instead.

Tips for Optimal Results

  • Node Type Matters: Adjacent nodes configured as Smooth or Symmetric will yield cleaner recalculations than nodes set to Cusp (corner) points. If the preserved curve appears slightly deformed after deletion, select the adjacent nodes and click the Make selected nodes smooth button on the top control bar before or after deleting the middle point.
  • Single vs. Multiple Nodes: Curve recalculation works best when deleting single nodes sequentially. Selecting a large span of nodes and deleting them simultaneously forces Inkscape to bridge a wide distance, which can result in noticeable profile shifts. Delete nodes one at a time for maximum precision.

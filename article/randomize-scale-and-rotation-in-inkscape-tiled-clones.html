Randomize Scale and Rotation in Inkscape Tiled Clones

Inkscape’s Create Tiled Clones tool is a powerful feature for generating complex patterns, textures, and distributions of vector objects. By leveraging the tool's built-in randomization settings, you can automatically vary the size and orientation of cloned objects to create natural, organic arrangements such as scattered leaves, starfields, or textured backgrounds. This guide details the exact steps and settings required to apply random scaling and rotation across a grid of tiled clones.

1. Prepare Your Base Object

  1. Open your project in Inkscape and select the object, path, or group you wish to clone.
  2. Ensure the object's rotation center is positioned where you want it. By default, it is in the center of the object, which is ideal for symmetrical rotations. If you want rotation around a different pivot (such as the base of a stem), click the object a second time to reveal the crosshair pivot point and drag it to your desired position.

2. Open the Create Tiled Clones Dialog

  1. Navigate to the top menu and select Edit > Clone > Create Tiled Clones....
  2. The Create Tiled Clones panel will open on the right side of the workspace.
  3. At the bottom of the panel, click the Reset button to clear any previous settings that might interfere with your new clone arrangement.

3. Configure the Clone Matrix Dimensions

At the bottom of the dialog, define the size of your distribution:

  • Select Rows, columns and enter the number of horizontal and vertical repetitions (e.g., 10 rows and 10 columns for 100 total objects).
  • Alternatively, select Width, height to fill a specific canvas dimension.

4. Randomize Object Scale

  1. Switch to the Scale tab in the Tiled Clones panel.
  2. Locate the Randomize % column at the far right.
  3. Enter a percentage in both the Scale X and Scale Y rows under the Randomize % column (for example, enter 50% to allow dimensions to vary up to 50% larger or smaller).
  4. If you want the objects to maintain their original aspect ratio without distorting, check the Uniform option.

5. Randomize Object Rotation

  1. Switch to the Rotation tab.
  2. Locate the Randomize % column.
  3. Enter your desired degree of variation in the Angle row under Randomize %.
    • Entering 360% or 180% will allow clones to rotate freely at full or semi-random angles.
    • Entering a lower value (such as 15% to 30%) applies subtle, slight variations.

6. Adjust Spacing and Overlap (Optional)

To prevent objects from strictly aligning to a rigid grid:

  1. Switch to the Shift tab.
  2. Add a percentage (such as 20% to 50%) in the Randomize % column for both Shift X and Shift Y to displace the positions randomly.

7. Generate and Refine the Clones

  1. Click the Create button at the bottom of the panel to generate your randomized clones.
  2. If the result needs adjustment:
    • Click Remove to delete the generated clones.
    • Modify the percentages in the Scale, Rotation, or Shift tabs.
    • Click Create again until you achieve the desired look.

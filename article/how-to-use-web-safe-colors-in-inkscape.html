How to Use Web-Safe Colors in Inkscape

This guide explains how to enable and apply the standard 216 Web-Safe color palette in Inkscape. By switching your active color swatches to the Web-Safe palette, you can quickly restrict your graphic fills and strokes to standard hex values that rely on combinations of 00, 33, 66, 99, CC, and FF.

Step 1: Locate the Palette Bar

At the very bottom of the standard Inkscape workspace, you will find the Color Palette bar, which displays a row of color swatches. To the far right edge of this bar, there is a small triangular arrow or menu icon.

Step 2: Switch to the Web-Safe Palette

  1. Click the small arrow icon at the far right of the bottom palette strip.
  2. In the context menu that opens, a list of pre-installed palettes will appear.
  3. Scroll through the list and select Web safe (or Web-Safe Colors, depending on your Inkscape version).
  4. The palette bar at the bottom will immediately update to show the 216 web-safe swatches.

Step 3: Apply Web-Safe Colors to Objects

Once the palette is active, you can apply colors directly to your vector artwork:

  • Set Fill: Select any vector object on your canvas and left-click on a color swatch in the bottom bar.
  • Set Stroke: Select the object, hold down the Shift key, and left-click on a swatch.

Step 4: Verify Values in the Fill and Stroke Dialog

To ensure your colors strictly adhere to standard web hex values:

  1. Open the Fill and Stroke panel by pressing Ctrl + Shift + F (or Cmd + Shift + F on macOS).
  2. Click on the Fill or Stroke paint tab.
  3. Choose the RGB or CMS tab depending on your version to view the RGBA or Hex field.
  4. Standard web-safe hex colors use repeating pairs of 00, 33, 66, 99, CC, or FF (for example: #336699 or #CC0033). When using the Web-Safe palette, every swatch clicked automatically applies one of these exact values.

How to Reset Zoom to 100% in Inkscape

This guide explains how to quickly reset your canvas zoom level to 100% actual size in Inkscape using keyboard shortcuts and menu navigation. Learning these view controls will streamline your vector design workflow, allowing you to quickly verify the true dimensions of your artwork and navigate complex projects with ease.

The Shortcut for 100% Zoom

The primary keyboard shortcut to reset the canvas zoom level to 100% (a 1:1 scale) in Inkscape is simply:

  • Press 1 on your keyboard (using the number row or the numeric keypad).

Pressing this key instantly snaps the current view to an exact 1:1 ratio, displaying your artwork at its real-world physical size relative to your display resolution.

Alternative Ways to Reset the Zoom

If you prefer using the mouse or interface menus, Inkscape provides several other ways to reset the view to 100%:

  • Via the Menu Bar: Go to the top menu and select View > Zoom > Zoom 1:1.
  • Via the Status Bar: Look at the bottom-right corner of the Inkscape window to locate the zoom percentage box. Double-click the current number, type 100, and press Enter. You can also right-click the zoom field to select 100% from the preset list.

Additional Helpful Zoom Shortcuts

Mastering complementary zoom shortcuts will make navigating your canvas much faster:

  • 2: Zooms out to 50% (1:2 ratio).
  • 3: Zooms directly to the currently selected object(s).
  • 4: Zooms to fit all drawn elements on the canvas within the window.
  • 5: Zooms to fit the entire page boundary within the window.
  • + / -: Incrementally zooms in or out from the center of the screen.

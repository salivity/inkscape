Convert Solid Color to Auto Palette Swatch in Inkscape

This guide explains how to convert an existing solid color into an Auto Palette swatch in Inkscape. By turning a standard flat fill into a document swatch, you can manage colors globally across your design, ensuring that any future change to the swatch instantly updates every linked object throughout your project.

Step 1: Select the Object

Using the Select Tool (S or F1), click on the object on your canvas that contains the solid color you want to convert into a swatch.

Step 2: Open the Fill and Stroke Dialog

Open the color properties by pressing Ctrl + Shift + F (or Cmd + Shift + F on macOS), or navigate to Object > Fill and Stroke from the top menu.

Step 3: Switch to the Swatch Paint Server

In the Fill and Stroke dialog:

  1. Choose either the Fill or Stroke paint tab, depending on which color you want to save.
  2. Look at the row of paint type icons directly beneath the tabs (Flat color, Linear gradient, Radial gradient, etc.).
  3. Click the Swatch icon (represented by a grid of colored tiles at the far right of the paint type list).

Inkscape will immediately convert the current flat color into a named document swatch.

Step 4: View and Use the Swatch in the Auto Palette

  1. Open the Swatches panel by pressing Ctrl + Shift + W or going to View > Swatches.
  2. Click the small arrow menu icon on the top-right of the palette bar or Swatches panel to select your palette.
  3. Choose Auto (or Document depending on your Inkscape version).
  4. Your newly converted color will now appear in the palette list.

You can now apply this swatch to any other object by selecting the object and clicking the swatch in your palette. Any adjustments made to the swatch's color values will update all linked objects simultaneously.

Move Shapes Between Sublayers in Inkscape

Managing complex vector illustrations requires organizing paths and shapes across different layer levels. In Inkscape, the unified Layers and Objects panel allows you to quickly reorganize artwork by dragging and dropping vector elements directly between layers and nested sublayers. This guide covers how to access the panel, expand nested structures, and correctly position your shapes within your document hierarchy.

1. Open the Layers and Objects Panel

To access your document hierarchy, open the unified panel by navigating to Layer > Layers and Objects... from the top menu, or by pressing the keyboard shortcut Ctrl + Shift + L (or Cmd + Shift + L on macOS). The panel will dock on the right side of your workspace.

2. Locate Your Shapes and Sublayers

In the panel list, layers are represented by folder icons, while individual vector shapes, paths, and groups are listed within them. If your sublayers or layers are collapsed:

  • Click the small arrow or chevron next to a parent layer to expand it and reveal its nested sublayers.
  • Expand the sublayer containing the vector shape you wish to move so that the specific object is visible in the tree.
  • Expand the destination sublayer so you can choose an exact placement inside it.

3. Drag and Drop the Shape

  1. Click and hold the target vector shape in the list within the Layers and Objects panel.
  2. Drag the item vertically toward the destination sublayer.
  3. Pay attention to the visual drop indicator line as you hover:
    • Hovering directly over a sublayer name: The sublayer highlights, indicating that dropping the shape will place it inside that sublayer (usually at the top of its stack).
    • Hovering between items within a sublayer: A horizontal line appears between existing objects or sublayers. Releasing the mouse button drops the vector shape into that exact position within the sublayer's stacking order.
  4. Release the mouse button to complete the move.

Alternative Move Method

If you have difficulty dragging within a crowded tree, you can move elements using the context menu:

  1. Select the vector shape on your canvas or in the panel.
  2. Right-click the shape and select Move to Layer... (or press Shift + M).
  3. A dialog box will appear listing all available layers and sublayers. Select your target sublayer from the list and click Move.

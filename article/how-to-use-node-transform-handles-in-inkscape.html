How to Use Node Transform Handles in Inkscape

Transforming individual or groups of nodes in Inkscape allows you to scale, rotate, and skew specific sections of a path without altering the entire object or switching back to the Select tool. By enabling the dedicated node transform handles in the Tool Controls bar, you can apply precise geometric transformations directly to any node selection.

1. Enable the Node Tool and Transform Handles

  1. Select the Node tool from the left toolbar, or press N on your keyboard.
  2. Look at the Tool Controls bar located horizontally across the top of the workspace.
  3. Locate and click the "Show transform handles for selected nodes" icon (an icon depicting a small bounding box with directional arrows around a node).

2. Select the Target Nodes

  1. Click on the vector path you want to edit so its nodes become visible.
  2. Select the specific nodes you wish to transform:
    • Box Select: Click and drag a selection rectangle over the target nodes.
    • Multiple Selection: Hold Shift and click each individual node you want to include.
  3. Once multiple nodes are selected, a rectangular bounding box with transform handles will appear around the selected nodes.

3. Transform the Node Selection

  • Scale: Click and drag any of the outer arrows on the corners or sides of the bounding box.
    • Hold Ctrl while dragging to maintain the proportional aspect ratio.
    • Hold Shift to scale outward or inward from the center point of the selection.
  • Rotate: Click once on any handle or selected node to switch the handles from scale arrows to rotation/skew arrows. Click and drag a corner arrow to rotate the selected nodes around the pivot point.
    • Drag the center + marker to move the rotation axis.
  • Skew: With the rotation handles active, click and drag the horizontal or vertical arrows located on the center of the bounding box edges to skew the nodes along that axis.
  • Move: Click and drag directly on any selected node to move the entire group across the canvas.

How to Delete Gradient Stops on Canvas in Inkscape

Managing gradients directly on the canvas in Inkscape allows for precise color transitions, but you may occasionally need to clean up unnecessary color stops. This guide explains the step-by-step process for selecting and removing intermediate color stops along a gradient line directly within your workspace using simple mouse and keyboard commands.

Step 1: Activate the Gradient Tool

Select your object, then switch to the Gradient Tool by clicking its icon in the left-hand toolbar or by pressing the shortcut key G. This action displays the gradient vector directly over the selected shape.

Step 2: Locate the Intermediate Stop

Look at the gradient line spanning across your object:

  • End stops are represented by a square at the start and a circle at the end.
  • Intermediate stops are represented by small diamond-shaped handles positioned along the line between the start and end points.

Step 3: Select the Stop

Click directly on the diamond-shaped intermediate stop you want to remove. Once selected, the stop will change color (typically highlighting in blue or red, depending on your theme) to indicate it is active.

Step 4: Delete the Stop

With the intermediate stop selected, remove it using either of these methods:

  • Keyboard: Press the Delete key or the Backspace key on your keyboard.
  • Tool Controls Bar: Click the Delete stop icon (a minus symbol next to a stop handle) located in the tool controls bar at the top of the screen.

The intermediate color stop will instantly disappear, and the gradient will smoothly interpolate between the remaining adjacent stops. Note that you can only delete intermediate stops; the terminal start and end handles cannot be deleted, as a gradient requires at least two points to exist.

Extract Unique Fill Colors to a Swatch in Inkscape

Managing a cohesive color scheme in complex vector projects often requires isolating the exact colors you have already applied across various paths. This guide provides a straightforward method for extracting all unique fill colors from an active Inkscape document, displaying them automatically through the built-in palette system, and arranging them into a dedicated visual swatch strip directly on your canvas for future sampling.

Step 1: Open the Swatches Panel and View Document Colors

Modern versions of Inkscape automatically track every unique color used across your document. You can access these without manually checking individual objects.

  1. Navigate to the top menu and select View > Swatches (or press Shift + Ctrl + W) to open the Swatches dockable panel.
  2. Click the small menu icon (a downward arrow or gear icon) located on the right side of the Palette bar at the bottom of the screen or inside the Swatches panel.
  3. Select Document (or Auto) from the list of available palette profiles.
  4. The palette will instantly refresh to display only the unique fill and stroke colors currently present in your active document.

Step 2: Convert Unique Fills to Permanent Swatches

To ensure your extracted fills act as globally linked swatches (which update across all objects if altered):

  1. Select an object containing a fill color you want to formalize.
  2. Open the Fill and Stroke panel by pressing Shift + Ctrl + F.
  3. Under the Fill tab, click the Swatch icon (the icon resembling a small color chart/ribbon, located next to the solid color and gradient icons).
  4. Inkscape assigns this color to the document's permanent paint servers, making it reusable across any element. Repeat this for any specific colors you need permanently tagged.

Step 3: Build an On-Canvas Visual Swatch Strip

If you require a visual swatch strip directly on the workspace for quick eye-dropper sampling or export:

  1. Press R to select the Rectangle Tool and draw a small square (e.g., 50px by 50px).
  2. With the square selected, duplicate it by pressing Ctrl + D. Drag the duplicate to the side. Repeat this duplication step until you have enough squares for each unique color shown in your Document Palette.
  3. Click on the first square, then left-click the first unique color block in the Document Palette at the bottom of your window.
  4. Select each subsequent square and assign the corresponding colors sequentially from your palette.
  5. Select all the squares together by dragging a selection marquee around them.
  6. Open the Align and Distribute panel (Shift + Ctrl + A).
  7. Under the Align section, click Center on horizontal axis (or vertical), and under the Distribute section, click Distribute horizontal space evenly.
  8. Press Ctrl + G to group the squares into a single, movable swatch strip.

Step 4: Export the Palette for Future Projects (Optional)

To carry these extracted colors into separate Inkscape files:

  1. In the Swatches panel, open the palette configuration menu.
  2. Choose Export Palette or select Save current palette.
  3. Save the palette file with a custom name. It will now appear permanently in your palette selector across all new documents.

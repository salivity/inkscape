Create an Auto Palette from Inkscape Colors

This guide explains how to automatically generate and configure a dynamic color palette based on the colors currently used in your Inkscape document. By using the built-in "Auto" palette feature, you can automatically extract every fill and stroke color from your artwork into a functional swatch bar, eliminating the need to manually sample and save individual colors.

Accessing the Palette Menu

Inkscape displays a color bar along the bottom of the workspace by default. To configure this bar to show colors from your active drawing:

  1. Locate the small arrow icon at the far right end of the bottom color palette bar.
  2. Click the arrow to open the list of available color preset palettes.
  3. Scroll to the top of the menu and select Auto.

Alternatively, you can open the dedicated Swatches dialog by navigating to View > Swatches (or pressing Shift + Ctrl + W). In the Swatches panel, click the menu icon in the upper-right corner and select Auto.

How the Auto Palette Operates

Once set to Auto, Inkscape scans your SVG file and compiles every distinct color applied to objects, paths, and strokes across all layers.

  • Dynamic Updating: As you introduce new colors to your artwork, the palette updates automatically to include them.
  • Selection: Clicking any swatch in the Auto palette applies that color as the fill to currently selected objects. Holding Shift while clicking applies the color to the stroke.
  • Removal: If you completely remove all objects using a specific color from your document, that color will disappear from the Auto palette upon the next canvas refresh or file save.

Customizing the Auto Palette Display

You can adjust how the generated swatches appear to better fit your workflow:

  1. Open the palette configuration menu by clicking the arrow at the right of the bottom palette or the menu icon in the Swatches panel.
  2. Select Configure... (or adjust settings directly in the drop-down menu depending on your Inkscape version).
  3. Adjust the following display settings:
    • Tile Size: Change the dimensions of the color swatches to make them easier to target.
    • Aspect Ratio: Adjust swatch shapes between square and wide rectangular tiles.
    • Rows: Increase the number of rows displayed at the bottom of the screen to view all active colors simultaneously without horizontal scrolling.

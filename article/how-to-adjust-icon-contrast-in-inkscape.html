How to Adjust Icon Contrast in Inkscape

This guide explains how to customize the icon appearance in Inkscape by switching between symbolic and multicolored icon styles and fine-tuning symbolic icon contrast. Adjusting these settings ensures your tools remain sharp, visible, and comfortable to work with, regardless of whether you use a light or dark user interface theme.

Accessing the Theming Menu

All icon and display adjustments are located within Inkscape’s global preferences.

  1. Open Inkscape.
  2. Navigate to Edit in the top menu and select Preferences (or press Ctrl + Shift + P on Windows/Linux, Cmd + Shift + P on macOS).
  3. In the left-hand sidebar of the Preferences window, expand the Interface section and click on Theming.

Switching Between Symbolic and Colored Styles

Inkscape allows you to quickly toggle between detailed, multicolored icons and flat, minimalist symbolic icons:

  1. Locate the Use symbolic icons checkbox under the Icon theme options.
  2. For Colored Icons: Uncheck Use symbolic icons. Inkscape will switch to standard multicolored icons, which provide maximum visual distinction across different tools using color coding.
  3. For Symbolic Icons: Check Use symbolic icons. Inkscape will render flat, single-color icons that integrate seamlessly with your desktop theme.

Adjusting Symbolic Icon Contrast

Symbolic icons adapt to your UI theme colors, but they can sometimes blend into the background or appear too faint. To adjust their contrast:

  1. In the Interface > Theming menu, ensure Use symbolic icons is enabled.
  2. Locate the Contrast slider beneath the icon options.
  3. Drag the slider to the right to increase icon contrast, making the icons bolder and brighter against dark backgrounds, or darker against light backgrounds.
  4. Drag the slider to the left to soften the icons if they appear overly sharp or distracting.
  5. If the icons still lack contrast, toggle the Use dark theme setting or change the base GTK theme dropdown to force Inkscape to recalculate the foreground and background luminance.

Changes typically take effect immediately. If the icons do not update right away, restart Inkscape to apply the new contrast and style configurations.

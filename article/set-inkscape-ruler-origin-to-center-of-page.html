Set Inkscape Ruler Origin to Center of Page

Setting the canvas ruler origin to the exact center of your active page in Inkscape changes your coordinate system so that (0,0) sits directly in the middle of your workspace, making symmetrical layouts and central radial designs much easier to manage. This guide walks you through configuring your snapping options to detect the page center and dragging the ruler origin point to snap directly onto it.

Step 1: Enable Page Center Snapping

To place the origin precisely at the center without manual guesswork, you must ensure Inkscape's snapping system is configured to detect page centers.

  1. Ensure the global Enable Snapping toggle is turned on (located at the top right of the application window or press %).
  2. Open the Snap Controls Bar or click the small popover arrow next to the snapping icon to expand advanced snapping settings.
  3. Under the Page snapping section, check the option for Snap to page center (or ensure that snapping to page centers and borders is active).

Step 2: Drag the Ruler Origin to the Center

The coordinate origin is controlled by the intersection square where the horizontal and vertical rulers meet at the top-left corner of the canvas.

  1. Locate the small square at the intersection of the horizontal and vertical rulers in the top-left corner of the workspace.
  2. Click and hold the left mouse button inside this intersection square. The cursor will change to a crosshair.
  3. Drag the cursor toward the center of your page.
  4. When the cursor approaches the middle of the page, the snapping guide will trigger with an indicator that reads Page center.
  5. Release the mouse button.

The ruler origin is now set to (0,0) at the center of the page. The horizontal ruler will read negative numbers to the left of the center and positive to the right, while the vertical ruler will read coordinates relative to this central origin.

How to Reset the Ruler Origin

If you ever need to restore the ruler origin back to its default page corner location:

  1. Move your cursor to the top-left intersection square between the two rulers.
  2. Double-click the intersection square.

The rulers will immediately reset to their default coordinate origin (top-left or bottom-left depending on your Inkscape version and preference settings).

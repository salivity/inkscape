How to Delete All Guides in Inkscape

Managing canvas alignment is an essential part of vector design, but accumulated guidelines can quickly clutter your workspace. Instead of manually dragging each guide off the canvas one by one, Inkscape provides a built-in command to clear them instantly. This guide explains how to remove every guideline in your document using a single action.

To delete all guides at once, follow these steps:

  1. Open your document in Inkscape.
  2. Navigate to the top menu bar and click on Edit.
  3. Scroll down and click Delete All Guides.

Once clicked, every horizontal, vertical, and angled guide on the canvas will be permanently removed immediately.

If your goal is simply to hide the guides temporarily rather than deleting them, you can toggle their visibility instead. Go to View > Guides or press the keyboard shortcut Shift + | (Shift + vertical bar). Using the toggle allows you to clear your visual workspace while preserving guide positions for later use. To permanently erase them, use the Delete All Guides option in the Edit menu.

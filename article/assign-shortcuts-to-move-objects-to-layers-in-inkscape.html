Assign Shortcuts to Move Objects to Layers in Inkscape

Customizing keyboard shortcuts in Inkscape can significantly accelerate your vector design workflow, particularly when organizing complex illustrations across multiple layers. This guide provides a direct, step-by-step walkthrough on how to set up custom hotkeys to move selected objects to layers above, below, or to a specific layer without opening context menus or layer dialogs.

Step 1: Open the Preferences Menu

Launch Inkscape and open the Preferences dialog. You can do this by navigating to Edit in the top menu and selecting Preferences, or by pressing the default shortcut Ctrl + Shift + P (Windows/Linux) or Cmd + Shift + P (macOS).

Step 2: Navigate to Keyboard Shortcuts

In the left sidebar of the Preferences window, click on the Interface category to expand it, then select Keyboard Shortcuts (or Keyboard depending on your Inkscape version). This panel lists every command available in Inkscape along with its current key binding.

Step 3: Search for Layer Move Commands

Use the search bar at the top of the Keyboard Shortcuts panel to locate the layer commands. Type Move selection or Layer into the search field. Look for the following actions under the Layers group:

  • Move selection to layer above
  • Move selection to layer below
  • Move selection to... (opens the prompt to choose any specific layer)

Step 4: Assign Your Custom Key Binding

  1. Click directly on the command you want to modify.
  2. Click in the Shortcut column next to the command name (the field will prompt you to press the new keys).
  3. Press the desired key combination on your keyboard (for example, Shift + Page Up for moving up or Shift + Page Down for moving down).
  4. If the shortcut is already in use by another action, Inkscape will display a conflict warning. You can choose to overwrite the existing shortcut or select a different combination.

Step 5: Test the Shortcut

Close the Preferences window. Create or open an Inkscape document with at least two layers. Select an object on your canvas and press your newly assigned shortcut key. The selected object will immediately shift to the targeted layer, confirming that your custom shortcut is active.

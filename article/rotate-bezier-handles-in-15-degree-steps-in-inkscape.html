Rotate Bezier Handles in 15-Degree Steps in Inkscape

Controlling Bezier handles with precision is essential for creating clean vector paths and smooth curves in Inkscape. While freehand adjustments offer flexibility, snapping handles to fixed angular increments ensures symmetry and exact angles across your design. In Inkscape, the primary shortcut key to rotate a Bezier handle in fixed 15-degree increments is the Ctrl key.

How to Use the Shortcut

To constrain the rotation of a Bezier handle to 15-degree steps, follow these steps:

  1. Select the Node Tool: Press N on your keyboard or select the "Edit paths by nodes" tool from the left-hand toolbar.
  2. Select the Node: Click on the path, then click directly on the node whose curve you want to adjust to reveal its Bezier handles.
  3. Hold Ctrl and Drag: Click and hold the circular end of the Bezier handle, press and hold the Ctrl key, and move your mouse.

As you move the cursor, the handle will snap to predefined angles radiating from the node, specifically at intervals of 15, 30, 45, 60, 75, and 90 degrees (and so on around the full 360-degree circle).

Customizing the Angular Increment

While 15 degrees is the default snapping angle in Inkscape, you can adjust this value to suit your project requirements:

  1. Open the Preferences dialog by pressing Ctrl + Shift + P (or navigating to Edit > Preferences).
  2. In the left panel, expand Behavior and select Steps.
  3. Locate the Rotation snaps every setting under the steps options.
  4. Change the value from 15 to your preferred degree measurement.

Once updated, holding Ctrl while manipulating handles will snap them using your custom increment instead of the default 15 degrees.

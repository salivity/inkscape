How to Rotate the Canvas View in Inkscape

This guide explains how to rotate the canvas view in Inkscape without altering the actual orientation or coordinates of your objects. Rotating the canvas is useful for drawing, sketching, and detailing artwork from comfortable angles, acting like rotating a physical sheet of paper while leaving your design geometry completely unchanged.

Method 1: Use the Status Bar (Most Precise)

  1. Look at the bottom-right corner of the Inkscape window.
  2. Locate the Rotation field, indicated by the letter R: followed by an angle value (e.g., 0.0°).
  3. Click the field and enter your desired rotation angle in degrees (e.g., 45 or -90), then press Enter.

Method 2: Mouse and Keyboard Shortcuts

  • Mouse Wheel: Hold Ctrl + Shift and scroll your mouse wheel up or down to rotate the canvas smoothly.
  • Keyboard Steps:
    • Press Ctrl + [ to rotate the canvas counter-clockwise by 15 degrees.
    • Press Ctrl + ] to rotate the canvas clockwise by 15 degrees.

Method 3: Touchpad Gestures

If you are using a multi-touch trackpad or a touch-enabled screen, place two fingers on the surface and twist them to rotate the canvas view freely.

How to Reset the Canvas Rotation to 0°

To return the canvas to its default orientation:

  • Double-click the R: rotation field in the bottom-right corner of the status bar.
  • Select the R: field, type 0, and press Enter.
  • Alternatively, go to the top menu and select View > Canvas Orientation > Reset Rotation (or press Ctrl + 1 to reset zoom and orientation depending on your Inkscape version configuration).

Flip Text on Path Without Inverting in Inkscape

Placing text along a curve or shape in Inkscape often causes the letters to appear upside down or mirrored when moved to the opposite side. This guide provides a straightforward method to switch text from one side of a path to the other while keeping the characters upright, legible, and properly oriented from left to right.

Method 1: Reversing Path Direction and Flipping

This is the most reliable method for circular paths and symmetrical curves.

  1. Attach the Text to the Path: Select both your text object and the path, then navigate to the top menu and select Text > Put on Path.
  2. Select Only the Path: Click on an empty area of your canvas to deselect everything, then click specifically on the path line (not the text).
  3. Reverse the Path Direction: With the path selected, go to Path > Reverse (or press Shift + R). This action moves the text to the inside or opposite side of the curve, though it may now appear upside down.
  4. Flip the Path: With the path still selected, flip it to correct the reading orientation:
    • For a text string located at the bottom of a circle or curve, click Object > Flip Horizontal (shortcut H) or Object > Flip Vertical (shortcut V), depending on your curve's orientation.
    • For complete circles, flipping both vertically and horizontally (or rotating 180 degrees) will place the text upright on the inside bottom edge.

Method 2: Adjusting the Baseline Shift

If you want the text to sit on the opposite side of the stroke without altering the path's underlying geometry or reversing its start/end points, use the baseline shift feature.

  1. Select the Text: Double-click your text object to enter text editing mode, then press Ctrl + A to select all characters.
  2. Adjust Baseline:
    • Hold down Alt and press the Down Arrow key to shift the characters below the path.
    • Hold down Alt and press the Up Arrow key to shift the characters above the path.
  3. Fine-tune in Font Settings: For exact measurements, open the Text and Font panel (Ctrl + Shift + T), switch to the Text tab, and adjust the Baseline shift value by entering a positive or negative numerical offset.

How to Draw a Perpendicular Bisector in Inkscape

Drawing a perpendicular bisector in Inkscape requires using the software's built-in geometric snapping features to identify the exact midpoint of an existing line segment and establish a true 90-degree angle. By configuring snapping for line midpoints and perpendicular paths, you can construct a mathematically precise bisector using native vector tools without manual measurement or calculations.

Step 1: Enable Geometric Snapping

To ensure the new line aligns accurately with the original line segment, configure the snapping controls:

  1. Turn on global snapping by clicking the Snap icon in the upper-right corner of the window (or press %).
  2. Open the Snap Controls menu (click the arrow next to the magnet icon or access the Snap Popover in newer Inkscape versions).
  3. Under Path Snapping, ensure Snap to paths is checked.
  4. Enable Snap to midpoints of line segments.
  5. Enable Snap perpendicularly to paths.

Step 2: Construct the Perpendicular Line

Once snapping is configured, you can draw the bisecting line directly using the Pen tool:

  1. Select the Draw Freehand Lines or Draw Bézier Curves and Straight Lines tool (press B).
  2. Position your cursor away from the line segment where you want the bisector to begin, and click once to place the first node.
  3. Move your cursor toward the target line segment.
  4. Hover near the center of the line segment until the snapping indicator displays both Perpendicular and Midpoint of line segment.
  5. Click to place the node, then press Enter to finish the path.

Alternative Method: Midpoint Snapping via 90-Degree Rotation

If you prefer constructing a centered bisector of identical length, you can use object duplication combined with midpoint snapping:

  1. Select the original line segment using the Select and Transform objects tool (S).
  2. Duplicate the line by pressing Ctrl + D.
  3. Rotate the duplicate line 90 degrees by clicking Object > Rotate 90° CW (or press ]).
  4. Ensure Snap to midpoints of line segments remains active in the Snap controls.
  5. Click and drag the rotated line segment by its midpoint until it snaps directly to the midpoint of the original segment.

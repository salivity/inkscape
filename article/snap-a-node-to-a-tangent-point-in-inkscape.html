Snap a Node to a Tangent Point in Inkscape

Snapping a node directly to the tangent point of an adjacent curved path in Inkscape allows you to create seamless transitions and precise geometric relationships between shapes. This guide outlines how to enable Inkscape’s advanced snapping controls, activate tangential snapping modes, and accurately position your nodes along adjacent curves without guesswork.

Step 1: Enable the Snapping System

  1. Locate the Snap Controls bar, which is typically found on the top-right corner of the workspace.
  2. Toggle the main Enable Snapping switch on (or press % on your keyboard).
  3. Click the dropdown arrow next to the snapping toggle to open the advanced snapping options popover.

Step 2: Configure Tangential Snapping

To ensure the node locks specifically to the tangent of a curve, specific snapping targets must be active:

  1. Under the snapping settings, ensure Paths snapping is enabled.
  2. Under the Advanced or Geometry section of the snapping menu, turn on Snap perpendicularly or tangentially.
  3. Ensure that Snap nodes, paths, and handles is also checked so Inkscape can register the node you are moving.

Step 3: Align the Node to the Curve

Once the correct snap modes are active, you can snap the node directly:

  1. Select the Node Tool (N) from the toolbox on the left.
  2. Click on the path containing the node you want to align to reveal its nodes and handles.
  3. Click and drag the node toward the adjacent curved path.
  4. Move the node along the contour of the target curve until a snap indicator appears. The tooltip will display Tangent to path or Perpendicular/tangent snap.
  5. Release the mouse button to lock the node directly onto the tangent point of the curve.

Alternative Method: Drawing Direct Tangents

If you are creating a new segment that must connect tangentially to an existing curve:

  1. Select the Draw Freehand Lines or Draw Bézier Curves and Straight Lines tool (B).
  2. Click the origin node or starting point on your canvas.
  3. Hover your cursor over the adjacent curved path until the snap marker displays Tangent to path.
  4. Click to create the node precisely on the curve's tangent point.

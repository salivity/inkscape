How to Reset Rotation Center in Inkscape

In Inkscape, moving an object's rotation center allows for custom pivot points, but you may frequently need to restore it to the object's true geometric midpoint. This guide provides the quick keyboard shortcut and step-by-step method required to instantly snap a modified rotation anchor back to the center of any shape or path.

The Quick Shortcut Method

The fastest way to reset the rotation center is using a simple mouse and keyboard combination:

  1. Activate the Select Tool: Press S or F1 on your keyboard, or click the black arrow icon in the left toolbar.
  2. Enter Rotation Mode: Click the object once to select it, then click it a second time. The transformation handles at the corners will change from straight resizing arrows to curved rotation arrows.
  3. Locate the Center Anchor: Look for the small crosshair (+) icon, which represents the current pivot point.
  4. Reset the Pivot: Hold down the Shift key and left-click directly on the crosshair anchor.

The crosshair will instantly jump back to the exact geometric center of the object's bounding box.

Alternative Method: Using Snapping

If you have trouble clicking the small crosshair directly, you can manually drag it back using Inkscape's snapping tools:

  1. Enable Snapping in the top-right toolbar (or press %).
  2. Make sure Snap to Object Midpoints and Snap to Bounding Box Centers are toggled on in the snapping popover menu.
  3. Click the object twice to reveal the crosshair pivot.
  4. Click and drag the crosshair toward the center of the object until the snapping indicator guides it into the exact midpoint, then release the mouse button.

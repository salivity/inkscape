Adjust 3D Box Vanishing Points in Inkscape

Adjusting the perspective convergence points—commonly known as vanishing points—for 3D boxes in Inkscape allows you to change the depth, angle, and realism of your 3D objects. This guide provides a straightforward walkthrough on how to locate, manipulate, and control the convergence points along the X, Y, and Z axes using Inkscape's built-in 3D Box tool.

1. Select the 3D Box Tool

To interact with the perspective settings of an existing 3D box, activate the 3D Box tool from the left toolbar or press the keyboard shortcut Shift + F4 (or simply X in newer versions).

2. Select the 3D Box

Click directly on the 3D box you wish to modify. Once selected, Inkscape displays the object's wireframe, perspective lines, and the vanishing points (represented by small X handles) extending along the X (red), Y (yellow), and Z (blue) axes.

3. Drag the Convergence Points

Locate the X marks at the ends of the perspective lines on your canvas:

  • Click and drag any X marker to change the position of that specific axis's vanishing point.
  • Moving the point closer to the box creates a dramatic, wide-angle perspective.
  • Moving the point further away creates a flatter, more orthographic perspective.

4. Toggle Parallel vs. Converging Perspective

You can toggle convergence on or off for each axis using the Tool Controls Bar at the top of the screen:

  • Look for the Angle X, Angle Y, and Angle Z options, along with their respective parallel projection buttons (indicated by parallel line icons).
  • Clicking the parallel toggle for an axis sends its vanishing point to infinity, making all lines along that axis perfectly parallel rather than converging to a single point.

5. Adjust Perspective Globally or Individually

  • Single Box Adjustment: Dragging the box's inner corner handles modifies the box's dimensions without moving the vanishing points.
  • Shared Perspective: Multiple 3D boxes drawn in the same document share the same vanishing points by default, allowing you to create consistent scenes. Holding Shift while dragging a vanishing point will move only that box's specific perspective lines without affecting other 3D objects.

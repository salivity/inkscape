Configure Touch Gesture Rotation in Inkscape

Configuring multi-touch gesture rotation in Inkscape allows digital artists to rotate the drawing canvas naturally using a compatible graphics tablet, screen display, or touch-sensitive device. This guide explains how to prepare your hardware drivers, configure Inkscape's internal canvas preferences, and troubleshoot input settings to achieve smooth, two-finger canvas rotation.

1. Enable Touch Input in Tablet Drivers

Before configuring Inkscape, ensure that multi-touch recognition is active at the driver level:

  1. Open your tablet's driver utility (such as Wacom Tablet Properties, Huion Tablet, or XP-Pen settings).
  2. Locate the Touch or Touch Options section.
  3. Verify that touch functionality is globally turned On.
  4. Check the gesture settings to ensure multi-finger gestures—specifically Rotate (two-finger twisting motion)—are enabled rather than overridden by application-specific shortcuts.
  5. If you are on Windows, ensure Use Windows Ink is toggled on in your tablet software, as modern Inkscape versions rely on native Windows Ink APIs for multi-touch gestures.

2. Configure Inkscape Canvas Preferences

Inkscape requires canvas rotation permissions to be enabled within its application settings:

  1. Launch Inkscape.
  2. Open the preferences dialog by going to Edit > Preferences (or Inkscape > Preferences on macOS), or press Ctrl + Shift + P.
  3. In the left navigation menu, expand Interface and select Canvas.
  4. Locate the Canvas interaction section.
  5. Ensure that the option labeled Allow canvas rotation is checked. If this is disabled, gesture rotation will be ignored.

3. Verify Hardware Input Devices

Inkscape must register your tablet as a valid multi-touch input device:

  1. In the Preferences window, select Input Devices.
  2. Click on Input Devices... to view the hardware list.
  3. Look for your tablet's touch module (often labeled "Touch", "Finger", or the vendor name).
  4. Ensure the device mode is set to Screen rather than Disabled.
  5. Save the configuration and close the dialog.

4. Performing and Resetting the Gesture

Once configured, place two fingers on your tablet's active surface and twist them clockwise or counterclockwise. Inkscape will rotate the workspace in real-time around the midpoint between your fingers.

To manage your view after rotating:

  • Reset Rotation: Press Ctrl + Shift + R to quickly snap the canvas back to the default 0-degree angle.
  • Manual Adjustment: You can also click and edit the numerical rotation value found in the lower-right corner of the status bar at the bottom of the Inkscape interface.

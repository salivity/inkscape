How to Lock a Path in Inkscape Objects Panel

Locking individual paths in Inkscape is an essential technique for preventing accidental selections, moves, or edits while working on complex vector artwork. This guide provides a straightforward walkthrough on how to open the Objects panel, locate a specific path within your document structure, and toggle its lock state on or off with a single click.

Step 1: Open the Objects Panel

To access your document's hierarchy, you must open the Objects (or Layers and Objects) dialog:

  1. Navigate to the top menu and click Object > Objects... (or Layer > Layers and Objects... depending on your Inkscape version).
  2. Alternatively, press the keyboard shortcut Ctrl + Shift + L (or Cmd + Shift + L on macOS) to open the panel on the right side of the workspace.

Step 2: Locate the Target Path

Once the panel is visible:

  1. Look through the list of layers and groups.
  2. Click the small arrow or plus icon next to a layer or group name to expand its contents and reveal the individual paths beneath it.
  3. Select your path directly on the canvas, which will automatically highlight the corresponding path entry in the Objects panel list.

Step 3: Toggle the Lock Icon

Next to each item listed in the panel, you will see status icons for visibility (an eye) and editability (a padlock):

  1. Move your cursor over the row corresponding to your selected path.
  2. Locate the Lock icon column on the right side of the path name. If the path is currently unlocked, the icon may appear open, dimmed, or only appear upon hovering over the area.
  3. Click the lock icon once to lock the path. The icon will change to a closed padlock, indicating the path cannot be selected or modified on the canvas.
  4. Click the closed lock icon again at any time to unlock the path and restore normal editing capabilities.

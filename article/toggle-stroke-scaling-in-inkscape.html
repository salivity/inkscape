Toggle Stroke Scaling in Inkscape

This guide explains how to toggle the global stroke scaling lock button on the Selector tool controls bar in Inkscape. By default, resizing an object can either stretch its stroke width proportionally or keep the stroke thickness constant. Learning how to toggle this setting allows you to control the exact appearance of your vector paths during transformation.

Steps to Toggle Stroke Scaling

  1. Activate the Selector Tool: Click the Select and transform objects icon (the black arrow) at the top of the left-hand toolbox, or press S (or F1) on your keyboard.
  2. Locate the Tool Controls Bar: Look at the horizontal toolbar situated directly above the main canvas and below the main menus.
  3. Find the Stroke Scaling Icon: Look toward the right side of the Selector controls bar. Find the button labeled "When scaling objects, scale the stroke width by the same proportion". The icon resembles a square with corner handles and lines indicating stroke thickness.
  4. Click to Toggle:
    • Active (Pressed In): When highlighted or depressed, stroke widths will scale up or down proportionally when you resize any selected object.
    • Inactive (Released): When turned off, resizing an object will alter its dimensions while leaving the stroke width at its original, fixed size.

Alternative Setting via Preferences

If the icon is hidden due to screen resolution or toolbar customization, you can toggle this behavior globally in Inkscape's settings:

  1. Open Edit > Preferences (or press Ctrl + Shift + P).
  2. Navigate to Behavior > Transforms in the left sidebar.
  3. Under the Transformations section, check or uncheck Scale stroke width.

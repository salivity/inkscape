Center Canvas on Mouse Cursor in Inkscape

In Inkscape, navigating your workspace efficiently is key to maintaining a smooth design workflow. This guide explains the specific command used to center your canvas view directly on the mouse cursor's current position, detailing the primary shortcut, how it works, and how to navigate if you do not have access to a standard middle mouse button.

To instantly center the canvas view on the current mouse pointer position without changing your current zoom level, click the Middle Mouse Button (pressing down on the scroll wheel).

Hover your cursor over the exact detail or object you want to focus on, and press the middle button once. Inkscape will immediately shift the viewport so that the point beneath your cursor moves to the dead center of the screen.

Alternative Methods and Workarounds

If your mouse lacks a middle button or you are using a laptop trackpad:

  • Two-Button Mouse Emulation: In many operating system settings, pressing both the left and right mouse buttons simultaneously registers as a middle click.
  • Canvas Panning with Spacebar: Hold down the Spacebar and move your mouse to quickly drag the canvas manually to your desired focal point.
  • Zoom-Centering via Keyboard: Pressing the + (plus) or - (minus) keys will zoom in or out while centering the view on the current position of the mouse pointer.
  • Zoom to Selection: Select the object you want to view and press 3 on your keyboard to center and zoom in directly on that specific element.

Delete Path Segment Between Two Nodes in Inkscape

This guide explains how to remove a specific path segment connecting two nodes in Inkscape without deleting the nodes themselves. By utilizing the Node tool and the dedicated segment deletion feature, you can open closed shapes, split paths, and adjust vector geometry while keeping your precise node positions completely intact.

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Activate the Node Tool Select the Edit paths by nodes tool from the left toolbar, or press the shortcut key N (or F2).

  2. Select the Path Click on the path you want to modify to display its nodes.

  3. Select the Segment or Nodes You have two ways to target the segment:

    • Direct Segment Click: Hover your cursor over the line segment connecting the two nodes. Click directly on the segment to select it. Both adjacent nodes will highlight to indicate the active segment.
    • Multi-Node Selection: Hold Shift and click both nodes that border the segment you want to remove, or click and drag a selection box around just those two nodes.

  4. Delete the Segment With the segment or both nodes selected, go to the Tool Controls Bar at the top of the workspace. Locate and click the Delete segment between two non-endpoint nodes button (an icon showing two nodes with a dashed or broken line between them).

    Alternatively, if you clicked directly on the segment itself, you can simply press the Backspace or Delete key on your keyboard.

The line segment between the two points will instantly disappear, leaving both original nodes in their exact locations as independent endpoints.

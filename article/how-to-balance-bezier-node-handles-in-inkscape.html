How to Balance Bezier Node Handles in Inkscape

Balancing the lengths of two opposing Bezier node handles without shifting their angle in Inkscape is an essential technique for creating smooth, uniform vector curves. This guide provides a straightforward method to equalize handle lengths accurately using Inkscape's built-in node types and modifier keys, ensuring the curve maintains its precise orientation while achieving perfect symmetry.

Step 1: Select the Node

  1. Switch to the Node Tool by pressing N or selecting it from the left-hand toolbox.
  2. Click on the path to reveal its nodes and handles.
  3. Click directly on the node whose handles you wish to balance.

Step 2: Convert to a Symmetric Node

The fastest and most precise way to equalize handle lengths without altering their angle is to convert the node into a symmetric node:

  1. With the node selected, press Shift + S on your keyboard.
  2. Alternatively, click the Make selected nodes symmetric icon in the Node Tool control bar along the top of the workspace.

When converted, Inkscape forces both handles to mirror each other in length while maintaining their existing shared angle, provided the handles were already opposing (collinear).

Step 3: Adjusting Length Without Shifting the Angle

If you need to extend or shorten the handles after balancing them while keeping their angle completely locked:

  1. Hover over either handle tip.
  2. Hold Ctrl and drag the handle along its current axis.
  3. Because the node is now set to Symmetric, adjusting the length of one handle automatically mirrors the exact same length onto the opposite handle. Holding Ctrl prevents any accidental deviation from the established angle.

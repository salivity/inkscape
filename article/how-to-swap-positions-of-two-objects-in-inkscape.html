How to Swap Positions of Two Objects in Inkscape

Swapping the spatial coordinates of two objects in Inkscape is a straightforward process that does not require manually copying and pasting X and Y coordinates. Using the native Align and Distribute panel, you can instantly exchange the locations of two selected elements with a single click. This guide details the exact steps to locate the swap tool and switch object placements accurately.

Step-by-Step Procedure

  1. Select the Objects

    • Activate the Select and Transform objects tool by pressing S or F1.
    • Click the first object, hold down the Shift key, and click the second object. Both elements must be selected simultaneously.

  2. Open the Align and Distribute Panel

    • Navigate to the top menu and select Object > Align and Distribute...
    • Alternatively, press the keyboard shortcut Ctrl + Shift + A (or Cmd + Shift + A on macOS).

  3. Locate the Rearrange Section

    • In the Align and Distribute dock on the right side of the workspace, locate the Rearrange section (you may need to expand it depending on your screen resolution and Inkscape version).

  4. Swap the Positions

    • Click the icon labeled Exchange positions of selected objects (or Swap positions). The icon typically depicts two opposing curved arrows or overlapping boxes switching places.
    • Upon clicking, Inkscape will instantly swap the spatial positions of the two chosen objects based on their geometric centers.

Alternative Manual Method (Coordinate Bar)

If you are working with an older version of Inkscape where the exchange button is unavailable:

  1. Select the first object and note its X and Y values displayed in the top Tool Controls bar.
  2. Select the second object, note its X and Y coordinates, and then change them to match the first object's original coordinates.
  3. Select the first object and enter the second object's original X and Y values.

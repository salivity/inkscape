How to Adjust Baseline Shift in Inkscape

Adjusting the vertical baseline shift in Inkscape allows you to position selected text characters above or below the standard baseline by an exact measurement. This guide covers how to select your target text, locate the baseline shift input field directly within the Text Tool Controls toolbar, set custom values in points, and apply the adjustment precisely.

Step 1: Select the Text

  1. Open your project in Inkscape.
  2. Select the Text tool from the left toolbox (or press T on your keyboard).
  3. Click and drag your cursor over the specific characters, words, or full line of text you want to adjust.

Step 2: Locate the Vertical Shift Field

Once the Text tool is active, the Tool Controls Bar at the top of the canvas displays typography settings:

  1. Look across the top toolbar past the font family, font size, and text alignment options.
  2. Find the icon showing a letter next to a vertical double-sided arrow. The tooltip for this field reads "Shift baseline of selected characters" or "Vertical shift".

Step 3: Enter a Custom Point Amount

  1. Click into the numeric entry box for the vertical baseline shift.
  2. Type your desired numerical value followed immediately by pt (for example, 4pt or -2.5pt). Inkscape automatically converts and scales units based on the document properties if points are specified.
  3. Use a positive value to shift the text downward, or a negative value (prefixed with a minus sign -) to shift the text upward.
  4. Press Enter to commit the change to the selected text.

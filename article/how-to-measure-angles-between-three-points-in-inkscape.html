How to Measure Angles Between Three Points in Inkscape

Measuring angles between arbitrary points is a fundamental task in vector design and technical drafting. This guide explains how to use Inkscape's built-in Measurement Tool to accurately determine the degree of an angle formed by any three coordinates on your canvas, ensuring precision using snapping and keyboard shortcuts.

Step 1: Enable Snapping (Optional but Recommended)

To measure between exact coordinates or object nodes, enable the Snapping feature located on the top-right toolbar (shortcut: %). Ensure snapping to nodes, paths, and intersections is active so the measurement endpoints snap securely to your three target points.

Step 2: Activate the Measurement Tool

Select the Measurement Tool from the left-hand toolbox (represented by a ruler icon) or press the keyboard shortcut M.

Step 3: Draw the Initial Ray

  1. Click and hold the mouse button on your first point (Point A).
  2. Drag the line to your middle point, which acts as the vertex (Point B).
  3. Release the mouse button. Inkscape will display the distance and the angle of this initial line relative to the canvas axis.

Step 4: Measure the Angle to the Third Point

  1. Hold down the Shift key.
  2. Click and drag the measurement endpoint located at the vertex (Point B) out toward your third target point (Point C).
  3. As you drag, an arc will appear between the original line segment (A–B) and the new line segment (B–C).
  4. Release the mouse button at Point C.

Step 5: Read the Angle

Look at the on-canvas Head-Up Display (HUD) near the vertex. Inkscape displays the exact interior and exterior angle measurements in degrees (°).

To reset or clear the measurement, simply click once anywhere on the canvas or switch to another tool by pressing S (Selector Tool).

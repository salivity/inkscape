How to Snap to Midpoints and Intersections in Inkscape

This guide explains how to enable and configure snapping to object midpoints and path intersections in Inkscape. By adjusting the global snap controls and activating specific node and bounding box options, you can achieve precise geometric alignment for complex vector illustrations and technical drawings.

1. Enable Global Snapping

Before you can snap to specific points, ensure that global snapping is turned on.

  • Look for the Snap Controls Bar, which is located at the top-right corner of the interface (or the right toolbar in older versions).
  • Click the top-most Toggle Snapping icon (a magnet) or press % on your keyboard to turn snapping on.

2. Snap to Path Intersections

Snapping to intersections allows your cursor, objects, or nodes to lock precisely onto the point where two paths cross.

  1. Open the Snap popover menu by clicking the arrow next to the snapping magnet icon.
  2. Under the Nodes section, ensure that Snap to paths is checked.
  3. Check the option labeled Snap to path intersections (represented by an icon with two crossing lines with a dot in the middle).

3. Snap to Midpoints

Inkscape allows you to snap to two different types of midpoints: bounding box midpoints and path segment midpoints.

Path Segment Midpoints

To snap directly to the center of a line or curved segment:

  1. Open the Snap popover menu.
  2. In the Nodes section, enable Snap to midpoint of lines/segments.

Bounding Box Midpoints

To snap to the midpoint of an object's overall outer boundary:

  1. Open the Snap popover menu.
  2. Enable Advanced Mode (if using Inkscape 1.2 or later).
  3. In the Bounding Boxes section, check Snap to midpoints of bounding box edges or Snap to bounding box centers.

4. Helpful Snapping Shortcuts

  • Temporary Disable: Hold the Shift key while dragging an object to temporarily bypass snapping.
  • Snap Sensitivity: Go to Edit > Preferences > Snapping to increase or decrease the snap distance threshold in pixels for better control.

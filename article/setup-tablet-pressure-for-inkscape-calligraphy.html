Setup Tablet Pressure for Inkscape Calligraphy

This guide provides a straightforward walkthrough for enabling and configuring graphics tablet pressure sensitivity for the Calligraphy tool in Inkscape. By properly configuring your hardware in Inkscape's input preferences and toggling the appropriate tool settings, you can achieve dynamic, variable-width strokes that respond naturally to your pen's pressure.

Step 1: Configure Your Tablet in Input Devices

Before Inkscape can read pressure data from your stylus, your graphics tablet must be recognized as an extended input device rather than a standard mouse.

  1. Connect your graphics tablet to your computer and ensure your tablet drivers are running.
  2. Open Inkscape and navigate to Edit > Input Devices....
  3. In the device list on the left, locate your tablet stylus (commonly listed as stylus, pen, or the brand name such as Wacom, Huion, or XP-Pen).
  4. Change the Mode dropdown menu from Disabled to Screen.
  5. Ensure the hardware axes (X, Y, and Pressure) are correctly assigned to their default values.
  6. Click Save and close the dialog.

Step 2: Select the Calligraphy Tool

  1. Choose the Calligraphy or Brush Stroke tool from the left toolbox, or press C (or Ctrl + F6) on your keyboard.
  2. The top toolbar will now display the Calligraphy Tool Controls.

Step 3: Enable Pen Pressure

  1. Look at the Tool Controls bar at the top of the screen.
  2. Locate the Pressure icon (represented by a pen tip with radiating force marks).
  3. Click the icon to activate it. When active, the button will appear highlighted or depressed.

Step 4: Adjust Stroke Dynamics

To make the pressure sensitivity clearly visible, adjust the following parameters in the Calligraphy Tool Controls:

  • Width: Sets the base stroke size. Set this to a moderate size (e.g., 30 to 50).
  • Thinning: Controls how much the stroke narrows with varying speed and pressure. A positive value (such as 10 to 40) creates a pronounced difference between light and heavy pressure.
  • Mass: Adds weight and smoothing to the stroke. Set this low (between 0 and 10) for instant response, or higher for stabilized lines.

Troubleshooting

  • No Pressure Detected: If lines remain a uniform width, open your tablet's manufacturer software and verify that Windows Ink (on Windows) is enabled, as Inkscape relies on standard API protocols to read pressure values.
  • Stroke Offset: If the stroke appears away from your cursor tip, return to Edit > Input Devices... and switch the Mode from Screen to Window.

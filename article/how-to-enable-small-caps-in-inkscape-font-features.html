How to Enable Small Caps in Inkscape Font Features

This guide explains how to access and apply OpenType small caps formatting to your text in Inkscape using the Font Features panel. By leveraging built-in font glyphs rather than scaled-down capital letters, you can achieve professional, balanced typography directly through the native Text and Font settings.

1. Ensure Font Compatibility

OpenType small caps require a font that includes dedicated small capital glyphs (such as EB Garamond, Alegreya, or Libertinus Serif). If a font lacks native OpenType small caps support, the feature will not render changes.

2. Select Your Text

  1. Choose the Select Tool (S) or the Text Tool (T) from the left toolbar.
  2. Click on the text object you want to modify, or highlight the specific letters using the Text Tool.

3. Open the Text and Font Dialog

  1. Navigate to the top menu and select Text > Text and Font....
  2. Alternatively, press the keyboard shortcut Shift + Ctrl + T.

4. Open the Features Tab

  1. In the Text and Font panel on the right, locate the three tabs at the top: Font, Text, and Features.
  2. Click on the Features tab to open the available OpenType settings for the active font.

5. Configure Small Caps

  1. Look through the list of feature categories for Capitals (OpenType tags smcp or c2sc).
  2. Open the dropdown menu next to the Capitals setting.
  3. Choose the desired formatting option:
    • Small capitals (smcp): Replaces standard lowercase letters with true small capitals while keeping regular uppercase letters unchanged.
    • All small capitals (c2sc): Converts both uppercase and lowercase letters into small capitals.

6. Apply the Changes

Click the Apply button in the bottom-right corner of the Text and Font dialog to render the small caps on your canvas.

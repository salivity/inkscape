Inkscape: Add Gradient to Stroke with Solid Fill

In Inkscape, you can style an object's stroke and fill independently, allowing you to apply a dynamic gradient to the border while maintaining a completely flat, solid color inside. This guide provides a straightforward method for isolating stroke properties using the Fill and Stroke dialog, applying linear or radial gradients exclusively to the outline, and adjusting the gradient handles without altering the underlying fill.

Step 1: Open the Fill and Stroke Panel

  1. Select the object on your canvas using the Select Tool (S or F1).
  2. Open the Fill and Stroke panel by pressing Shift + Ctrl + F (Windows/Linux) or Shift + Cmd + F (macOS), or by navigating to Object > Fill and Stroke in the top menu.

Step 2: Confirm the Solid Fill

  1. In the Fill and Stroke panel, click on the Fill tab.
  2. Ensure the Flat color icon (the solid square icon) is selected.
  3. Choose your desired solid color using the color wheel, sliders, or the palette at the bottom of the screen.

Step 3: Apply the Gradient to the Stroke

  1. Switch to the Stroke paint tab in the same panel.
  2. Select either the Linear gradient icon or the Radial gradient icon (located next to the flat color icon).
  3. Applying the gradient here ensures only the stroke receives the gradient, leaving the solid fill intact.
  4. If your stroke is not visible, switch to the Stroke style tab and increase the Width value.

Step 4: Customize the Gradient

  1. Activate the Gradient Tool by pressing G or selecting it from the left-hand toolbar.
  2. On your canvas, you will see a gradient line with control handles over your object.
  3. Click the starting node (square) to select it, then choose a color from your palette.
  4. Click the ending node (circle) to assign the secondary color.
  5. Click and drag the nodes to change the angle, length, and position of the stroke's gradient. You can double-click along the gradient line to add additional color stops as needed.

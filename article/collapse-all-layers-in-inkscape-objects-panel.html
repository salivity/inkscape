Collapse All Layers in Inkscape Objects Panel

Managing complex vector graphics in Inkscape often leads to a cluttered workspace filled with deeply nested groups and expanded sublayers. This guide outlines the fastest methods to collapse all expanded layer folders in Inkscape's Layers and Objects panel, allowing you to instantly reset your project hierarchy, reduce visual clutter, and navigate your document more efficiently.

Method 1: The Multi-Selection Keyboard Shortcut (Fastest Overall)

The quickest way to collapse all top-level layers and their nested contents at once is using keyboard navigation within the panel:

  1. Open the Layers and Objects panel by pressing Ctrl + Shift + L (or Cmd + Shift + L on macOS).
  2. Click inside the panel list to give it keyboard focus.
  3. Press Ctrl + A (or Cmd + A on macOS) to select every item in the document tree.
  4. Press the Left Arrow key on your keyboard.

Pressing the Left Arrow key once collapses all selected items that are currently expanded. If deeply nested items remain open within their parent folders, pressing the Left Arrow key a second time will collapse them entirely up to the root level.

Method 2: Recursive Shift-Click

If your project is organized under a single root layer or you want to collapse specific complex groups instantly:

  1. Locate the parent layer or group folder in the Layers and Objects panel.
  2. Hold down the Shift key on your keyboard.
  3. Click the small dropdown arrow (caret) next to the folder name.

Holding Shift while clicking the collapse arrow forces Inkscape to apply the collapse action recursively to every child group and sublayer contained within that parent, immediately closing the entire branch.

How to Adjust Word Spacing in Inkscape

Adjusting typography in Inkscape allows you to fine-tune readability and visual impact by modifying how individual characters and full words sit next to one another. Inkscape features dedicated, independent controls for both letter spacing (tracking) and word spacing directly within the Text tool settings. This guide explains how to access these separate controls and adjust your word spacing without affecting your letter spacing.

Step 1: Activate the Text Tool

  1. Open your document in Inkscape.
  2. Select the Text tool from the left toolbar, or press the shortcut T (or F8).
  3. Click and drag across the specific text you want to modify, or click anywhere inside the text frame to apply changes to the entire text block.

Step 2: Locate the Text Controls Bar

When the Text tool is active, a contextual control bar appears horizontally across the top of the workspace. This bar contains font families, sizes, alignments, and several numeric input fields for advanced kerning and spacing.

Step 3: Adjust Word Spacing Independently

Look closely at the numeric inputs on the right side of the Text controls bar:

  1. Letter Spacing Field: Represented by an icon showing two letters with an arrow between them (A|A or AA). This field controls tracking across all individual characters. Leave this field unchanged if you only want to adjust the distance between words.
  2. Word Spacing Field: Located directly next to the letter spacing field, represented by an icon showing two distinct blocks or words with an arrow between them (often depicted as A B or A_A).
  3. Click inside the Word Spacing box and enter your desired value, or use the up and down arrows to increase or decrease the spacing.
  4. You can also change the measurement unit (e.g., px, pt, em, %) using the unit dropdown adjacent to the spacing controls to achieve relative or absolute spacing.

Keyboard Shortcuts for Fine-Tuning

If you prefer using keyboard shortcuts while typing or editing:

  • Adjust Letter Spacing (Tracking): Select your text and press Alt + > to increase tracking, or Alt + < to decrease it.
  • Adjust Word Spacing: Inkscape does not assign a default single-key shortcut for word spacing alone, meaning the dedicated numeric field on the top control bar is the most precise method to adjust word spacing separately from character spacing.

How to Project Isometric Views in Inkscape

Projecting flat 2D orthographic drawings (top, front, and side views) into a pseudo-3D isometric projection in Inkscape is achieved using the SSR method: Scale, Shear, and Rotate. By taking advantage of Inkscape’s native Transform panel, you can mathematically transform flat vector artwork into precise 30-degree isometric planes. This guide walks you through the exact values and steps required to convert each orthographic view and assemble them into a cohesive isometric illustration.

Preparing Your Artwork and Tools

Before projecting, ensure each orthographic view (top, front, and side) is grouped individually (Ctrl + G) so paths, strokes, and fills transform together.

  1. Open the Transform panel by navigating to Object > Transform or pressing Shift + Ctrl + M.
  2. Familiarize yourself with the three relevant tabs in this panel: Scale, Shear, and Rotate.
  3. In the Scale tab, ensure the Scale proportionally checkbox is unticked, and set the unit dropdown to %.
  4. In the Shear and Rotate tabs, make sure the angle unit is set to degrees (°).

Always execute the three operations in strict order: Scale, then Shear, then Rotate. Applying them out of sequence will distort the geometry incorrectly.

Projecting the Top View

The top view represents the horizontal plane seen from above, resting between the 30-degree and 150-degree isometric axes.

  1. Select your grouped Top orthographic artwork.
  2. Scale: Go to the Scale tab. Set Width to 100% and Height to 86.602%. Click Apply.
  3. Shear: Switch to the Shear tab. Set Horizontal shear to -30° and Vertical shear to 0°. Click Apply.
  4. Rotate: Switch to the Rotate tab. Set the angle to 30° counter-clockwise (positive value). Click Apply.

Projecting the Front (Left) View

The front view forms the left-facing vertical surface of your isometric object, where vertical lines remain vertical and horizontal lines angle downwards to the left at 30 degrees.

  1. Select your grouped Front orthographic artwork.
  2. Scale: In the Scale tab, set Width to 100% and Height to 86.602%. Click Apply.
  3. Shear: In the Shear tab, set Horizontal shear to -30° and Vertical shear to 0°. Click Apply.
  4. Rotate: In the Rotate tab, set the angle to -30° (clockwise rotation, or 330°). Click Apply.

Projecting the Side (Right) View

The side view forms the right-facing vertical surface, where vertical lines remain vertical and horizontal lines rise to the right at 30 degrees.

  1. Select your grouped Side orthographic artwork.
  2. Scale: In the Scale tab, set Width to 100% and Height to 86.602%. Click Apply.
  3. Shear: In the Shear tab, set Horizontal shear to 30° and Vertical shear to 0°. Click Apply.
  4. Rotate: In the Rotate tab, set the angle to 30° counter-clockwise. Click Apply.

Assembling the Projected Views

Once all three planes have been transformed:

  1. Enable snapping on the right toolbar (% or click the magnet icon).
  2. Activate Snap to paths, Snap to cusps nodes, and Snap to corners of bounding boxes.
  3. Drag the projected Front, Side, and Top groups together using the Select and Transform objects tool (S). Align their matching corners so the surfaces snap securely into a solid isometric form.

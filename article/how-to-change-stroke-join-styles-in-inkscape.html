How to Change Stroke Join Styles in Inkscape

In Inkscape, adjusting the stroke join style determines how corners and path vertices connect, allowing you to switch between sharp (Miter), smooth (Round), and flattened (Bevel) corners. This guide provides a straightforward walkthrough on how to access the Fill and Stroke settings and apply the appropriate join style to any vector path.

Step 1: Open the Fill and Stroke Dialog

  1. Launch Inkscape and open your project.
  2. Open the Fill and Stroke panel by pressing Ctrl + Shift + F (Windows/Linux) or Cmd + Shift + F (macOS). Alternatively, go to the top menu and select Object > Fill and Stroke.

Step 2: Select Your Object

  1. Choose the Select and Transform Objects tool (S) or the Edit paths by nodes tool (N) from the left toolbar.
  2. Click on the path or shape whose corner style you want to change. Ensure the object has a stroke applied with a width greater than 0 so the join changes are visible.

Step 3: Switch to the Stroke Style Tab

  1. In the Fill and Stroke panel on the right, click on the Stroke style tab.
  2. Look for the Join section, which features three distinct icons representing each join type.

Step 4: Select Your Join Style

Click on one of the three join buttons depending on the desired corner appearance:

  • Miter Join (First Icon): Creates sharp, pointed corners extending to the intersection of the outer stroke lines. When using Miter Join, you can adjust the Miter limit value. If the corner angle is sharper than the miter limit, Inkscape automatically converts it to a bevel to prevent excessively long spikes.
  • Round Join (Second Icon): Softens corners by rounding off the intersection of the strokes with a circular arc, creating a smooth transition.
  • Bevel Join (Third Icon): Chops off the pointed corners flat, creating a squared, beveled edge across the vertex.

The selected style applies immediately to all vertices on the active path.

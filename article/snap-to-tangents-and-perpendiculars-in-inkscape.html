Snap to Tangents and Perpendiculars in Inkscape

This guide explains how to enable and use tangent and perpendicular path snapping in Inkscape. By configuring Inkscape's advanced snapping controls and using the Bezier Pen tool, you can create precise geometric alignments, draw lines perfectly normal to curved surfaces, and align objects along exact tangent vectors without manual calculations.

Step 1: Enable the Snapping System

  1. Locate the Snap Controls bar, which is situated in the top-right corner of the interface (in Inkscape 1.2 and newer) or along the right edge of the screen (in earlier versions).
  2. Toggle the master Enable Snapping switch to the ON position (Shortcut: %).

Step 2: Configure Advanced Snapping Options

  1. Click the dropdown arrow next to the master snapping toggle to open the detailed snapping popover menu.
  2. Switch the mode from Simple to Advanced if prompted.
  3. Under the Paths or Geometry section, ensure the following settings are enabled:
    • Snap to paths: Allows the cursor to interact with path outlines.
    • Snap perpendicularly: Snaps your cursor at a strict 90-degree angle to any path or curve segment.
    • Snap tangentially: Snaps your cursor along the tangent vector of a path or curved segment.

Step 3: Drawing Perpendicular Lines

  1. Select the Draw Bezier curves and straight lines tool (Shortcut: B).
  2. Click anywhere on your canvas to establish the starting node of your line.
  3. Move your cursor toward the target path or curve.
  4. Hover near the target path until the snapping tooltip appears, indicating "Perpendicular to path".
  5. Click to set the node. The resulting line will meet the target curve at an exact 90-degree angle.

Step 4: Drawing Tangent Lines

  1. Select the Bezier Tool (B).
  2. Click on the canvas or an existing node to begin the line.
  3. Hover your cursor over the curved path or circle you wish to intersect tangentially.
  4. Move the cursor along the curve until the snap indicator displays "Tangential to path".
  5. Click to confirm the point. The segment will align flush against the curve's perimeter without crossing its boundary at the contact point.

Tips for Precise Snapping

  • Adjust Snapping Distance: If snapping triggers too easily or not easily enough, go to Edit > Preferences > Behavior > Snapping and adjust the Snap sensitivity slider.
  • Temporary Disable: Hold Shift while dragging or drawing to temporarily suspend all active snapping if you need to place a node close to a snap point without locking to it.

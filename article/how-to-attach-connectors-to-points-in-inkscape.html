How to Attach Connectors to Points in Inkscape

Inkscape provides a dedicated Diagram Connector tool that binds lines dynamically to magnetic connection points and geometric nodes on vector shapes. This guide explains how to enable snapping, establish precise connection targets on your objects, draw dynamic connectors, and adjust routing settings so that lines stay attached even when shapes are moved around the canvas.

1. Activate the Diagram Connector Tool

  1. Open your project in Inkscape.
  2. Select the Create diagram connectors tool from the left toolbar or press the keyboard shortcut O (or Ctrl + F11 / F11 depending on your Inkscape version).

2. Configure Snapping for Magnetic Points

To ensure connector endpoints lock onto specific target locations rather than general object bounds, configure Inkscape's snapping controls:

  1. Toggle Enable Snapping on the top-right toolbar (or press %).
  2. Open the Advanced Snapping popover to enable:
    • Snap to nodes, paths, and handles
    • Snap to object centers and midpoints
    • Snap to path intersections

These snapping rules act as magnetic anchors for connector endpoints.

3. Define Custom Connection Points on Shapes

By default, the Connector tool snaps to the center of an object. To attach connectors to custom perimeter locations:

  1. Select your shape and convert it to a path by going to Path > Object to Path (Shift + Ctrl + C).
  2. Switch to the Edit paths by nodes tool (N).
  3. Double-click on any segment along the shape’s perimeter to insert a new node at the exact location where you want the connector to attach. These nodes serve as custom magnetic anchors.

4. Draw and Attach the Connector

  1. Switch back to the Diagram Connector tool (O).
  2. Hover over your starting shape until the red or green handle appears at the desired node or center point.
  3. Click and drag the connector line toward the target shape.
  4. Hover over the target shape's custom node or midpoint until it highlights with a snap indicator, then release the mouse button.

Once locked, the connector endpoint changes appearance to indicate it is securely tethered to that magnetic point.

5. Adjust Connector Routing and Behavior

Use the tool control bar at the top of the window to modify connector properties:

  • Avoid Overlapping Shapes: Click the Make connectors avoid selected objects button to automatically route lines around intermediate objects.
  • Orthogonal vs. Polyline: Toggle between straight polyline connectors and orthogonal (right-angled) connectors to match your diagram style.
  • Line Length and Curvature: Adjust the connector distance and curvature settings directly in the controls bar for automated line spacing.

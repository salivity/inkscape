How to Dock Dialog Panels into Tabs in Inkscape

Managing your workspace in Inkscape can significantly improve your design workflow, especially when working with multiple tools simultaneously. Inkscape allows you to group individual floating dialogs—such as Fill and Stroke, Align and Distribute, and Layers—into a single, compact, tabbed notebook docked to the side of your screen. This guide explains how to combine, dock, and organize these floating dialogs into a clean, tabbed interface.

1. Open the Dialogs You Need

Open the tools you plan to use from the top menu or via keyboard shortcuts. Common dialogs include:

  • Fill and Stroke: Ctrl + Shift + F (or Object > Fill and Stroke)
  • Align and Distribute: Ctrl + Shift + A (or Object > Align and Distribute)
  • Layers and Objects: Ctrl + Shift + L (or Layer > Layers and Objects)

If these panels appear as floating windows on your canvas, they are ready to be docked together.

2. Dock the First Dialog

If you do not already have a docked sidebar:

  1. Click and hold the title bar of the first floating dialog.
  2. Drag it toward the far right edge (or left edge) of the Inkscape window.
  3. A blue highlight area will appear along the edge, indicating the docking drop zone.
  4. Release the mouse button to dock the dialog into the side panel.

3. Combine Floating Dialogs into a Tabbed Notebook

To turn the docked panel into a multi-tabbed notebook:

  1. Click and hold the tab header or the title bar of a second floating dialog.
  2. Drag the dialog directly over the center or the header area of the already-docked dialog panel.
  3. Look for the blue highlight zone that covers the center of the existing panel rather than its top, bottom, or side borders.
  4. Release the mouse button.

The two panels will merge into a single notebook frame, displaying tabs at the top or side that allow you to switch between them. Repeat this drag-and-drop process for any additional dialogs you wish to include in the notebook.

4. Rearrange or Undock Tabs

  • Reorder Tabs: Click and drag any tab label along the tab strip to change its position relative to other tabs.
  • Undock a Dialog: Click and drag a specific tab away from the notebook into the open canvas area to turn it back into a floating panel.
  • Collapse the Dock: Click the small arrow icon at the top of the dock column to collapse the entire tabbed notebook into a thin vertical strip, maximizing your canvas space while keeping your tools accessible.

How to Toggle Font Fractions in Inkscape

OpenType fonts often include built-in fraction ligatures that convert standard numbers separated by a slash (such as 1/2) into stylized, proportional fractions. Inkscape provides access to these advanced typographic settings through its Font Features interface. This guide outlines the exact steps to locate the Font Features panel and toggle fraction ligatures on and off for your text elements.

Step 1: Open the Text and Font Dialog

To access OpenType controls, you must open the main text formatting panel:

  1. Go to the top menu and select Text > Text and Font...
  2. Alternatively, use the keyboard shortcut Shift + Ctrl + T (Windows/Linux) or Shift + Cmd + T (macOS).

Step 2: Select Your Text

  1. Choose the Text tool (represented by the "T" icon in the left-hand toolbar, or press T on your keyboard).
  2. Click and highlight the specific portion of text containing the fraction, or use the Selector tool (S) to select the entire text object.

Step 3: Navigate to the Features Tab

  1. In the Text and Font dialog panel docked on the right side of the screen, look at the tabs at the top.
  2. Click on the Features tab (located next to the "Font" tab).

Step 4: Toggle Fraction Ligatures

  1. Scroll through the available OpenType feature categories until you find the Numbers section.
  2. Locate the Fractions option (the OpenType feature tag is frac).
  3. Check the box to turn fraction ligatures on, or uncheck the box to turn them off.
  4. Click the Apply button at the bottom right of the dialog to save your changes to the selected text.

Font Compatibility Note

If the Fractions checkbox is grayed out, missing, or produces no visual change after clicking Apply, the currently selected font does not contain OpenType fraction tables. To use this feature, switch to a font that explicitly supports advanced OpenType typography, such as EB Garamond, Montserrat, or Adobe Caslon Pro.

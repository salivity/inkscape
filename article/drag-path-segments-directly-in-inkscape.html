Drag Path Segments Directly in Inkscape

Inkscape allows you to shape curves intuitively by directly manipulating path segments instead of manually adjusting individual Bézier control handles. This guide explains how to activate the Node tool, click and drag any line or curve directly on the canvas, and utilize modifier keys to control curve geometry quickly and accurately.

Step 1: Select the Node Tool

Open your document and ensure the path you want to modify is on the canvas. Activate the Edit paths by nodes tool by clicking its icon in the left-hand toolbox or by pressing the keyboard shortcut N (or F2).

Step 2: Select the Path

Click on the path you wish to edit. The path’s outline will be highlighted, and its existing nodes will become visible as small squares, diamonds, or circles.

Step 3: Hover Over the Path Segment

Move your cursor directly over the line or curve between two nodes. Notice that the cursor changes: a small curved line or hand icon appears next to the pointer arrow, indicating that the segment is ready to be manipulated directly.

Step 4: Click and Drag the Curve

Click and hold the left mouse button directly on the segment, then drag it in the direction you want the curve to bend. As you move the mouse:

  • Inkscape automatically generates or updates the Bézier control handles for the adjacent nodes.
  • Straight lines automatically convert into smooth curves.
  • The shape changes dynamically in real time relative to where along the segment you clicked and pulled.

Keyboard Modifiers for Direct Dragging

You can refine how the path bends by using modifier keys while dragging:

  • Shift + Drag: Dragging a segment while holding Shift preserves the positions of the opposite handle lines, allowing you to stretch or distort the curve without altering the existing tangent angles at the end nodes.
  • Ctrl + Drag: Holding Ctrl while manipulating paths can constrain angles or limit handle movement depending on your specific tool preferences and snap settings.

Connect Two Paths with a Smooth Curve in Inkscape

Connecting two separate open paths in Inkscape typically results in a straight connecting line by default. This guide walks through the process of combining two independent paths and bridging their open endpoints with a smooth, adjustable curved segment rather than a rigid straight line, using Inkscape's built-in Node Tool functionality.

Step 1: Combine the Separate Paths

Before you can link nodes from two different paths, Inkscape requires them to belong to the same path object.

  1. Select the Selection Tool (S or F1).
  2. Hold Shift and click on both paths to select them simultaneously.
  3. Go to the top menu and select Path > Combine (or press Ctrl + K).

Step 2: Join the Endnodes with a Segment

Once combined, you need to create the bridge between the two endpoints.

  1. Switch to the Node Tool (N or F2).
  2. Click and drag a selection box over the two open endpoints you wish to connect, or hold Shift and click both nodes.
  3. In the Node Tool control bar at the top of the screen, click the Join selected endnodes with a new segment icon (it looks like two separate endpoints connected by a line).

This action creates a straight line connecting the two paths.

Step 3: Bend the Segment into a Smooth Curve

You can convert the straight bridging segment into a curve instantly without changing node types first.

  1. With the Node Tool still active, hover your cursor directly over the middle of the newly created straight line. The cursor will change to a hand with a curve indicator.
  2. Click and drag the segment outward. The straight line will immediately flex into a smooth Bezier curve.

Step 4: Make the Connection Seamless

To ensure the new curve transitions smoothly into the original paths without sharp corners:

  1. Select the two nodes at either end of the newly curved segment.
  2. In the top Node Tool bar, click Make selected nodes smooth (or press Shift + S).
  3. Retract or extend the circular node handles that appear to balance the curvature and achieve the desired shape.

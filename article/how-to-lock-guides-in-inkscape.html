How to Lock Guides in Inkscape

Accidentally moving guides while designing can disrupt alignment and slow down your workflow. This guide provides a straightforward walkthrough on how to lock your guides in Inkscape—both globally and individually—to prevent unwanted dragging and maintain a precise layout on your canvas.

Method 1: Lock All Guides via Document Properties

To lock every guide on your canvas at once:

  1. Open your Inkscape project.
  2. Navigate to File > Document Properties in the top menu, or press the keyboard shortcut Shift + Ctrl + D.
  3. In the dialog window that appears on the right, switch to the Guides tab.
  4. Check the box labeled Lock all guides.
  5. Close the Document Properties panel.

All existing guides and any new guides created will now be locked in place, making them impossible to select or drag accidentally.

Method 2: Lock an Individual Guide

If you only want to secure specific guides while keeping others movable:

  1. Double-click directly on the guide line you want to secure to open the Guide Properties dialog.
  2. Check the Locked checkbox.
  3. Click OK (or Apply).

How to Unlock Guides

To make your guides editable again:

  • For all guides: Open Document Properties (Shift + Ctrl + D), go to the Guides tab, and uncheck Lock all guides.
  • For a single guide: Double-click the locked guide line to open its properties, uncheck Locked, and click OK.

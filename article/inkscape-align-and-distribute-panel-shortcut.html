Inkscape Align and Distribute Panel Shortcut

The Align and Distribute panel is one of the most frequently used tools in Inkscape, allowing designers to position and space vector objects with precision. This guide covers the default keyboard shortcut used to access the panel instantly on Windows, Linux, and macOS, alternative navigation methods, and the core alignment modes available to streamline your workflow.

The Keyboard Shortcut

To open the Align and Distribute panel immediately in Inkscape, press the following key combination on your keyboard:

  • Windows & Linux: Ctrl + Shift + A
  • macOS: Cmd + Shift + A

Pressing this combination instantly toggles or opens the dockable Align and Distribute dialog on the right side of the workspace without navigating through menus.

Menu Alternative

If you prefer using the mouse, you can open the panel through the application menu:

  1. Click on Object in the top menu bar.
  2. Select Align and Distribute... from the dropdown list.

You can also click the Align and Distribute icon located on the command bar along the top or right side of the screen, depending on your interface layout.

How to Use the Panel

Once the panel is open, alignment is controlled by three main steps:

  1. Select Objects: Use the Selector tool (S or F1) to select two or more objects you wish to arrange.
  2. Choose the Reference ("Relative to"): Use the Relative to dropdown menu to choose what the objects will align against. Common anchors include:
    • Selection Area: Uses the collective bounding box of all selected items.
    • First selected: Keeps the first selected object in place and moves the rest.
    • Last selected: Keeps the most recently selected object stationary.
    • Page: Aligns objects relative to the page boundaries.
    • Drawing: Aligns objects relative to the bounding box of all artwork in the document.
  3. Apply Alignment or Distribution:
    • Align: Click the relevant icon to align edges (left, right, top, bottom) or center points (horizontal or vertical).
    • Distribute: Click the distribution icons to arrange objects with equal spacing between their edges or centers.
    • Rearrange: Use the grid arrangement or overlap removal tools at the bottom of the panel to space complex groups evenly.

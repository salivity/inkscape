Close Gaps with Paint Bucket in Inkscape

This guide explains how to automatically close visible gaps when using the Paint Bucket tool in Inkscape. By adjusting the built-in gap closure settings, you can fill open line art and incomplete vector boundaries instantly without having to manually join path nodes or redraw lines.

Steps to Automatically Close Gaps

  1. Select the Paint Bucket Tool
    Click the Fill Bounded Areas icon (Paint Bucket) in the left-hand toolbar, or press Shift + F7 (or U depending on your version).

  2. Locate the "Close gaps" Setting
    Look at the Tool Controls Bar at the top of the canvas. Find the dropdown menu labeled Close gaps.

  3. Choose a Gap Closure Size
    Click the Close gaps dropdown and select the setting that matches the size of the gaps in your artwork:

    • None: Only fills completely closed areas.
    • Small: Bridges tiny pixel-level breaks in paths.
    • Medium: Bridges standard gaps in hand-drawn or sketched line art.
    • Large: Bridges significant openings between paths.

  4. Click to Fill the Area
    Click inside the unclosed boundary. Inkscape will automatically calculate the missing boundary segment and generate a filled path.

Additional Tips for Better Gap Filling

  • Adjust the Threshold: If the fill bleeds through despite using the "Large" gap setting, lower the Threshold value in the top controls bar to make the color detection more strict.
  • Use Grow/Shrink: To eliminate any thin white borders between the fill and the stroke, set the Grow/shrink by field to a small positive value (e.g., 0.5 px to 1.0 px).
  • Zoom Level: Inkscape's Paint Bucket is a pixel-based operation on the current screen view. Zooming out slightly can make gaps appear smaller to the tool, allowing it to bridge larger openings more easily.

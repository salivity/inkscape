Configure Inkscape Mouse Scroll for Pan and Zoom

This article provides a straightforward guide on how to configure your mouse scroll wheel behavior in Inkscape to switch between canvas panning and zooming. You will learn how to adjust Inkscape’s internal preference settings to make zooming the default scroll action, as well as how to utilize built-in modifier keys to pan and zoom quickly.

Changing Scroll Wheel Behavior in Preferences

By default, Inkscape uses the mouse scroll wheel to pan the canvas vertically. If you prefer the scroll wheel to zoom without holding down any keyboard keys, change the setting via the Preferences menu:

  1. Open the Preferences window by going to Edit > Preferences from the top menu, or by pressing Ctrl + Shift + P (Windows/Linux) or Cmd + Shift + P (macOS).
  2. In the left-hand navigation pane, expand the Interface category and select Canvas.
  3. Locate the navigation settings section.
  4. Check the box labeled Zoom with mouse wheel (or select "Zoom" under the mouse wheel behavior dropdown, depending on your Inkscape version).
  5. Close the Preferences dialog. The changes are saved automatically.

Once enabled, scrolling up zooms in and scrolling down zooms out.

Default Keyboard Modifiers for Panning and Zooming

If you keep the default configuration, you can navigate the canvas using the following mouse wheel and keyboard combinations:

  • Vertical Pan: Rotate the Mouse Wheel up or down.
  • Horizontal Pan: Hold Shift and rotate the Mouse Wheel.
  • Zoom In / Zoom Out: Hold Ctrl (or Cmd on macOS) and rotate the Mouse Wheel.
  • Free Pan (Canvas Drag): Click and hold the Middle Mouse Button (scroll wheel click) or hold the Spacebar while moving the mouse to drag the canvas freely in any direction.

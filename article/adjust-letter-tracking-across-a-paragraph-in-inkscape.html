Adjust Letter Tracking Across a Paragraph in Inkscape

Adjusting letter tracking—the uniform spacing between all characters across a block of text—is an essential typography technique in vector design. In Inkscape, tracking cannot be applied globally by simply selecting a text frame with the Selection tool; instead, it requires highlighting the target text with the Text tool. This guide explains how to quickly select an entire paragraph and use either the Tool Controls bar or keyboard shortcuts to modify tracking simultaneously across the entire selection.

Step 1: Activate the Text Tool

Select the Text tool from the toolbox on the left side of your canvas, or press T (or F8) on your keyboard.

Step 2: Select the Entire Paragraph

Click inside the text frame containing the paragraph you want to modify. Press Ctrl + A to select all the text within the frame. If you only want to track a specific paragraph within a larger multi-paragraph text object, click and drag your cursor over that specific paragraph to highlight it completely.

Step 3: Adjust Tracking Using the Controls Bar

With the text highlighted, look at the Tool Controls bar directly above the canvas workspace:

  1. Locate the Spacing between letters field (represented by an icon showing characters with horizontal arrows between them, typically labeled AA or an arrow between two letters).
  2. Enter your desired tracking value into the input field. Positive numbers increase the spacing between letters, while negative numbers tighten it.
  3. Choose your preferred unit of measurement from the drop-down menu next to the field (such as px, pt, or em). Using em is often recommended for typography, as it scales proportionally with the font size.
  4. Press Enter to apply the changes across the entire highlighted paragraph.

Step 4: Adjust Tracking Using Keyboard Shortcuts

For quick, visual micro-adjustments without manually typing values:

  1. Keep the entire paragraph highlighted.
  2. Hold Alt and press the Right Arrow key to expand the tracking (increase letter spacing).
  3. Hold Alt and press the Left Arrow key to tighten the tracking (decrease letter spacing).
  4. Hold Alt + Shift while pressing the Left or Right Arrow keys to make tracking adjustments in larger increments.

To reset the tracking back to the default font spacing, highlight the paragraph, type 0 into the letter spacing field on the Tool Controls bar, and press Enter.

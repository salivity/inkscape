Export TIFF with LZW Compression in Inkscape

Exporting vector artwork to a high-resolution raster TIFF file while managing file size is an essential workflow for print and digital publishing. In Inkscape, this process involves defining your target resolution in dots per inch (DPI) or pixel dimensions, choosing the TIFF format, and specifying LZW (Lempel-Ziv-Welch) lossless compression in the format-specific export settings.

Step 1: Open the Export Panel

  1. Open your project in Inkscape.
  2. Open the Export dialog by pressing Ctrl + Shift + E (Windows/Linux) or Cmd + Shift + E (macOS), or navigate to File > Export in the top menu.

Step 2: Configure the Export Resolution

  1. In the Export dialog, choose your export area tab: Document, Page, Selection, or Custom.
  2. Locate the DPI (Dots Per Inch) field.
  3. Enter your desired resolution. For standard high-quality printing, set this value to 300 DPI or higher. For screen-based applications, 96 or 72 DPI is standard.
  4. Adjusting the DPI will automatically update the pixel width and height fields while preserving the physical dimensions of your document.

Step 3: Select the TIFF File Format

  1. At the bottom of the Export panel, locate the file type dropdown menu (which defaults to PNG).
  2. Click the dropdown and select TIFF (*.tiff) from the list of available raster formats.
  3. Choose your destination folder and enter a filename for the output file.

Step 4: Apply LZW Compression

  1. Click the Export button.
  2. A format-specific parameter window titled TIFF Options (or similar depending on your Inkscape version) will appear.
  3. Locate the Compression dropdown menu.
  4. Select lzw from the list of compression algorithms.
  5. Confirm that other color settings (such as bit depth or color profile) meet your requirements.
  6. Click OK to process and save the file.

The resulting TIFF file will maintain your specified resolution without visual artifacts, packaged with lossless LZW compression to reduce the overall disk footprint.
